# BNN timing method (R.G. Rao) — distilled

All quotes are from `docs/bhrigu-nandi-nadi.pdf` (page-text; line refs are into the
extracted text, indicative). This is the canonical method the engine's Nāḍī timing
must follow.

## 1. The method in Rao's own words (reader instructions)
> "Take the causative i.e. **kāraka planet** of the particular aspect you want to
> study, consider the **adjacent houses of that planet and the seventh house
> therefrom.** Then … you will also be able to make correct predictions."

And the reading factors around that kāraka:
> "the **sign occupied** by a planet, the **Lord of the sign**, the **other planets
> posited in that sign**, the planets in the **7th and 12th** to the planet … The
> **exchange of signs** (parivartana) … In addition the **transit of Jupiter** …
> He gives the results of the sign he transits."

So a matter is read from its **kāraka**, its **adjacent (conjunct) planets**, the
**7th-from-kāraka** (the partner/counter seat), the **12th**, the sign-lord,
co-tenants and sign-exchanges — and **timed by Jupiter's transit**.

## 2. The event kārakas (Rao's list)
> "we take into account: (1) **Jupiter for births of children**, (2) **Mercury for
> education**, (3) **Saturn for profession**, (4) **Venus for marriages**,
> (5) Jupiter's transit over the sign occupied by Ketu for pilgrimages."

(For a female chart the **husband** is additionally read from **Mars** — the Nandi
refinement — but the marriage *event* kāraka is **Venus** universally.)

## 3. The timer = JUPITER's transit ("rounds")
Jupiter = **jeeva** (the native). He moves ~1 sign/year → a full **round ≈ 12
years**; over a life he makes ~6-7 rounds. As Jupiter **transits/contacts** each
natal planet, that planet's matter fructifies:
> "Jupiter represents the native, and in his transits he crosses (1) Mars, (2) Rahu,
> … indicates the ages at which the native … obtains happiness … marriage, birth of
> children etc."

**MARRIAGE = Jupiter transits / contacts VENUS** (or a Venus-owned sign
Libra/Taurus, or Venus's natal star). Grounded examples:
- "Jupiter … contacts Venus in Virgo … around the native's 30th year, **marriage is
  likely to take place**."
- "When Jupiter transits Venus at about [the] 20th year …" (marriage).
- "when Jupiter transits the **star occupied by Venus** at birth, his wife …"
- "entry into **Libra owned by Venus**, indicate that marriage …"

## 4. SATURN — the karma / "double-approval" planet
> "Saturn is the **Lord of Karma**. He is neither good nor bad … His transit
> indicates the **changes**."

Saturn's contact with the kāraka also brings the event — and is the **delay/late**
signature:
- "When **Saturn transits Capricorn his conjunction with Venus will bring about the
  marriage** of the native."
- Classical double-rule (confirmed by research): for a **timely** marriage **natal
  Jupiter** is in activation mode; for a **late** marriage **natal Saturn** is in
  activation mode. → An event matures most surely when **BOTH Jupiter and Saturn**
  connect the kāraka ("double approval").

## 5. Second / multiple marriage
Read from the chart directly — the book times successive marriages separately:
> "the first marriage will take place during his 33rd year and the **second marriage
> will take place** during his [later] …"
(Engine: our ordinal → Nth-marriage axis; BNN chain → benefic after a Ketu-cut.)

## 6. Precision
Timing is by the sign/star Jupiter (and Saturn) transits; **degree-to-degree /
star-level** matching sharpens it ("transits the star occupied by Venus"). Degree
locks are a **refinement on top of** the Jupiter/Saturn contact — not the primary
gate.
