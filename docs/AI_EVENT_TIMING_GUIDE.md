# AI Event-Timing Interpretation Guide

*How the AI reads the engine's timing **data** into a dated call. The engine
computes; this document is the judgment layer. It is descriptive and
event-agnostic — no native dates, no chart-specific hardcoding (blind-test
integrity). Companion to `AI_TRIANGULATION_PROMPT.md`; this one is timing-only.*

---

## Cardinal rule — the engine gives data, you give the verdict

Never read a **salience / convergence score as the answer.** In testing, the
ranking put the *wrong* window on top in every single timing case, while the
correct window sat lower. Scores, "systems firing", leaderboards — all are
**inputs**, never the call. The reading below is yours to do by hand.

## Pick the tool to the question

| Question type | Engine | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Will it happen? Will it last? Afflicted? | **KP** (cuspal sub-lord) | promise / denial / quality — KP's strength |
| **When** does it happen? | **Daśā (Vimśottari + Jaimini Chara) × Gochara double-transit** | KP broad-fires on timing; the daśā of the event's own significators is the clock |

KP is a *quality/yes-no* instrument. Do **not** time an event with it.

---

## The timing procedure — follow in order

**1. Derive the delivery mechanism from the natal chart FIRST — before any window.**
Name *which significator delivers this event*, and *through what period*:
   - the **bhāva lord** (7th for marriage, 10th/6th for job, 4th for vehicle/home…);
   - **its dispositor**, if the lord is **debilitated or weak** — a debilitated
     significator delivers in its **dispositor's** daśā, not its own;
   - the **kāraka** (Venus for marriage/vehicle, Jupiter for a woman's husband,
     Saturn/Sun for career…) and the **Darakaraka / relevant chara-kāraka**;
   - note **promise & tempo** from KP (does the cusp sub-lord promise it; is the
     tempo early / on-time / late).

**2. Candidate dates = the bhāva double-transit windows.**
List every Jupiter+Saturn **double-transit window on the matter's house** (and its
lord). **These are the candidate event dates.** The double-transit on the bhāva is
a *primary trigger*, not a mere confirmation (see Principle A).

**3. Overlay each significator's daśā (MD>AD>PD) onto those windows.**
Mark which windows also carry a significator's daśā — **lord AND kāraka; do not
demand they co-fire** (Principle B). A candidate window needs *a* significator
running, not the "ideal" one.

**4. Read the pratyantar (PD) lord's nature at each candidate — delivery vs
obstruction.** A live window with a benefic / significator PD **delivers**; the
same window with a **malefic or Saturn PD delays, obstructs, or cancels**. Flag
such windows as *"activity that may not consummate"* (Principle F).

**5. Weight life-stage, and scan the FULL plausible range.** Do not call an event
that contradicts the native's life stage (Principle G). Do not cap the search
below the plausible range — *especially* when the natal read says "late."

**6. Choose.** Pick by the **tightest convergence** of (bhāva double-transit) ×
(a significator's daśā), quality-checked by the PD lord. Give a **primary and a
secondary** window. Name the daśā and the transit at each. Never let a score decide.

## Principles (the hard-won ones)

**A. The bhāva double-transit is a PRIMARY trigger, not a confirmation.**
Repeatedly, the event fired on the double-transit of the matter's *house* even
when the "ideal" significator was **not** in the antardaśā. Treat every
double-transit window on the bhāva as a candidate date in its own right.

**B. Do not over-constrain.** Adding a condition the event does not need —
"wait for the house-lord's AD *too*", "the kāraka *and* the lord must co-fire" —
pushes the call late. The single most common failure mode. Take the *loosest*
sufficient signature: one significator's daśā + the bhāva double-transit.

**C. Do not privilege lord-vs-kāraka rigidly.** Some matters deliver on the
**house-lord's** period (the lord carries it); kāraka-heavy matters (e.g. Venus
for marriage/vehicle, running a long mahādaśā) deliver on the **kāraka's**
period. Let the transit convergence decide which, not a fixed rule.

**D. Weak / debilitated significators deliver via their DISPOSITOR.** When the
bhāva lord is debilitated or afflicted, the delivering period is its
**dispositor's** daśā/antardaśā (the dispositor "carries" the fallen lord),
often with the fallen lord itself surfacing at the pratyantar.

**E. Events cluster at daśā-change ONSETS.** A new mahādaśā or antardaśā of a
significator is a life-chapter trigger; the event frequently lands within weeks
of the AD/MD **onset**, not mid-period.

**F. Read the PD lord's nature — a live window can yield a prospect that fails.**
Astrology flags marital/career **activity**; whether it *consummates* depends on
the finer factors. A significator window with a **malefic/Saturn pratyantar**
often produces an *attempt that breaks* (a prospect discussed then cancelled), not
a completed event. Report it as such, do not upgrade it to the finished event.

**G. Respect life-stage and house-meaning.** A lit career-house (10th) during the
**education** years (4th/5th/9th active, a Ketu/skill daśā) is a *student*, not an
employee — do not call it a "job start." A first salaried job wants the
**6th (service) / 2nd–11th (earned income)**, not the 10th alone. Match the houses
to the *specific* life-event, and never call an event that the native's age /
stage makes implausible.

**H. Two-body events (marriage) can be triggered from the partner's chart.** A
marriage fires when **both** charts are lit; the "loudest" factor may live on the
*partner's* chart (their significator antardaśā) while this chart shows the
7th-lord period and a **transit node on the lagna/kāraka**. Do not force a single
chart to carry the whole signature.

**I. Rāhu/Ketu = the "sudden / unconventional" flavour.** A node as Darakaraka, or
**transit Rāhu/Ketu on the lagna or the kāraka**, colours the event sudden,
fast-moving, or non-traditional — a *quality* read on top of the timing.

## Anti-patterns (what went wrong before, so it does not recur)

- Reading the **salience leaderboard** as the answer → wrong window, every time.
- **Over-constraining** on the significator AD → event fired on the bhāva
  double-transit a year earlier/later than the "ideal" daśā call.
- Mistaking a **student's** lit 10th house for a job start (ignored life-stage).
- Calling a **malefic-PD** window the finished event when it was a **cancelled**
  attempt (ignored the pratyantar's nature).
- **Capping the scan range** below the plausible span after the natal read said
  "late."
- Timing with **KP** (broad-fires) instead of the daśā × double-transit clock.

---

*Operational summary:* mechanism first → the bhāva double-transits are the
candidate dates → overlay any significator's daśā (don't over-constrain) →
quality-check the PD lord → weight life-stage → pick the tightest convergence,
primary + secondary. The score is never the verdict.
