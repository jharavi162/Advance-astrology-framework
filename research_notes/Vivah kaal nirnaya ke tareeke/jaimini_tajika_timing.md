## Q1. Which Chara or Narayana daśā periods give marriage (Jaimini Upadeśa Sūtras, Sanjay Rath, K.N. Rao, Iranganti Rangacharya)?

### Takeaway
The Jaimini Sūtras do not contain an explicit "marriage comes in the daśā of sign X" sutra. The sutras give the **Upapada (UL)** and **2nd-from-UL** doctrine for the existence, quality, plurality and loss of a spouse. They also give one daśā rule about the spouse: wife's death falls in the **Śūla daśās (1st/5th/9th) from Jupiter**. Every rule that says "this sign's daśā gives marriage" is commentarial or practitioner material:
- Sanjay Rath: the daśā sign linked to Venus or Ketu; a daśā sign aspected by the Dārakāraka (DK); in the Navāṁśa Nārāyaṇa daśā, association with the Navāṁśa UL.
- Iranganti Rangacharya: an 11-item list built from the 7th from Lagna or AL, DK, UL, A7 and Venus.
- Gautam Dave: Saturn, Mars and Jupiter transiting UL, AL and the 7th, plus DK argala on the Chara MD and AD signs.

### Cited Findings

**A. Primary Jaimini sutra material (B. Suryanarain Rao, *Jaiminisutras*, 1955 ed.)** — [archive.org: Jaiminisutras1955EditionByBSRao](https://archive.org/details/Jaiminisutras1955EditionByBSRao)
- **[PRIMARY]** Upapada definition: "Su. 1.—Upapadam padam pitranucharat." The UL is the pada (ārūḍha) of the house that follows the Lagna's anucara, i.e. the 12th. B.S. Rao notes that some commentators instead take the ārūḍha of the 12th-lord's position, and that the two readings can give different signs. Inputs: Lagna, 12th lord's sign, ārūḍha rule. Computability: C, but the exception convention must be chosen. [B.S. Rao pp. ~108–109](https://archive.org/details/Jaiminisutras1955EditionByBSRao)
- **[PRIMARY]** "Su. 2.—Tatra papasya papayoge pravrajya daranasova." B.S. Rao renders it: "If the Upapada has evil conjunction or aspect or if the 2nd from it has an evil planet, the wife will die or he will embrace pravrajya." Su. 3: the Sun in the 2nd from UL is not counted as a malefic. Su. 4 "Shubhadrigyoganna": benefic aspect or conjunction cancels the result. Rao's note says the sutra means death does not happen "during those evil times indicated by the planets". So he reads these as conditions that become active in a period. [B.S. Rao p. 109–110](https://archive.org/details/Jaiminisutras1955EditionByBSRao)
- **[PRIMARY]** "Su. 5.—Neechey daranasaha." If the 2nd from UL is in a debilitated navāṁśa or holds a debilitated planet, "there will surely be death to wife in that evil period." [B.S. Rao p. 110](https://archive.org/details/Jaiminisutras1955EditionByBSRao)
- **[PRIMARY — plurality / second marriage]** "Su. 6.—Ucchey bahudaraha": if the 2nd from UL holds an exalted planet or falls in an exalted navāṁśa, the person "will have many wives." "Su. 7.—Yugme cha": if the 2nd from UL falls in Mithuna (yugma), the person has many wives. "Su. 8.—Tatra swamiyukte swarkshe va … uttarayushi nirdaraha": if the 2nd from UL is joined by the Ātmakāraka or by its own lord, the person has no wife in the latter part of life. Rao glosses "Mithuna" for *yugma*; another reading of *yugma* is "an even or dual sign". Computability: C (sign tests). [B.S. Rao p. 111](https://archive.org/details/Jaiminisutras1955EditionByBSRao)
- **[PRIMARY]** "Su. 23.—Saptamamsagrahebhyaschaivam": the same results may be predicted from the 7th from UL, from the Kalatra-kārakāṁśa, and from the lords of both. [B.S. Rao p. 116](https://archive.org/details/Jaiminisutras1955EditionByBSRao)
- **[PRIMARY — spouse daśā rule]** "Su. 10.—Gurusule kalatrasya": "The Dasas of 1st, 5th or 9th Rasis from the position of Guru will cause the death of his wife." Rao's note: "He names three Dasas called Shoolas. The worst among them will kill the wife." Su. 11 generalises this to the Śūla daśās from the relevant kāraka. This timing rule concerns loss of the spouse. For a second marriage it marks when the first bond ends, not when the second marriage happens. Inputs: Jupiter's sign; a rāśi daśā (Śūla daśā in Rao's context). Computability: C, once the daśā system is fixed. [B.S. Rao pp. 159–160](https://archive.org/details/Jaiminisutras1955EditionByBSRao)

### Inferences
- The rules that recur across authors are: a daśā or antardaśā sign that holds, aspects, or is the 7th from (a) the DK, (b) the UL or 2nd-from-UL, (c) the A7, (d) Venus or Ketu, (e) the 7th from Lagna or AL. All of these are deterministic sign and rāśi-dṛṣṭi relations, so an engine could emit them as DATA. One example is a per-daśā-sign flag set such as {contains_DK, aspected_by_DK, is_UL, aspects_UL, is_2nd_from_UL, is_A7, contains_Venus, contains_Ketu, is_7th_from_AL, is_7th_from_AK}, with no score attached.
- The main convention risk is which Chara daśā variant is used (K.N. Rao, Neelakantha, Raghava Bhatta/Nrisimha Suri, or Rath's Nārāyaṇa), because they give different sign sequences. The engine should name its variant.
- UL computation has two readings (B.S. Rao's note), and *yugma* in "Yugme cha" is ambiguous. Both are convention choices to log, not rules to tune.

### Gaps
- I found no primary Jaimini sutra that names the marriage daśā sign. Rath's *Jaimini Maharishi's Upadesa Sutra* (his translation, with commentary) was not reachable as text.
- I could not locate K.N. Rao's *Predicting through Jaimini's Chara Dasha* on archive.org, so his exact marriage rules (often summarised as "a sign related to the DK or UL in MD/AD, plus the Jupiter–Saturn double transit over the related signs") are **unverified** here.
- Karakāṁśa-based timing: only Rath's statement on the 7th from kārakāṁśa was found; I found no sutra-level timing rule.

## Q2. Tajika: how is the marriage year identified, and what are the exact Vivāha and Punarvivāha Sāham formulas?

### Takeaway
Primary text: *Tājika Nīlakaṇṭhī* (TN), Sāhama-adhyāya verse 17, with Sitaram Jha's Hindi commentary. It gives **Vivāha Sāham = Venus − Saturn (+ Lagna), *always*, the same by day and night (सततं).** B.V. Raman's *Varshaphal* agrees: "Day or Night — Sukra's long — Sani's long". **TN's list of 50 sāhams contains no "Punarvivāha" sāham.** The marriage year is identified from: the Vivāha Sāham and its lord in Ithasāla with benefics; the Lagna lord and 7th lord in Ithasāla; Venus as year lord; the natal Venus sign falling in the varṣa 7th; and similar varṣa-7th-bhāva combinations. Muntha in the 7th is read as **adverse** for the spouse, not as a marriage trigger.

### Cited Findings
**Sāham formulas**
- **[PRIMARY]** TN Sāhama 17: "शनेर्दिवार्कं निशि चन्द्रमार्केर्विशोध्य सूर्येन्दुभनाथयोगात् । स्यात्कार्यसिद्धिः **सततं विशोध्य मन्दं सितात् स्यात्तु विवाहसद्म** ॥१७॥" Jha's bhāṣya: "सर्वदा शनि को शुक्र में घटाकर पूर्ववत् (लग्न जोड़ने से) विवाह सहम होता है", i.e. *always* subtract Saturn from Venus and add the Lagna. — [Tajika Neelakanthi, Gudhartha Prakashika comm. by Sitaram Jha (archive.org)](https://archive.org/details/nxfu-tajika-neelakanthi-gudhartha-prakashika-commentary)
  - **Formula:** Vivāha = Venus − Saturn + Asc (mod 360). Add 30° ("सैक") when the Lagna does *not* fall between the subtrahend (Saturn) and the minuend (Venus). This general proviso is stated in TN verse 5 (Puṇya): "शोध्यशुद्धाश्रयभान्तराले लग्नं न चेत्सैकभमेतदुक्तम्".
  - Inputs: sidereal longitudes of Venus, Saturn and Asc in the annual (varṣa-praveśa) chart. Computability: C.
- **[PRIMARY/translation]** B.V. Raman, *Varshaphal or the Hindu Progressed Horoscope*, §73: "VIVAHA SAHAM — Day or Night — Sukra's long — Sani's long". §64 gives the general rule: "an amount of 30° must be added if from the position of the minuend to that of subtrahend, there is no lagna, while the longitude of lagna is always added." Raman counts 50 sāhams per Nīlakaṇṭha, 48 per Venkatesa and 21 per Keśava. — [archive.org: VarshaphalOrTheHinduProgressedHoroscope](https://archive.org/details/VarshaphalOrTheHinduProgressedHoroscope)
- **[PRIMARY]** TN Sāhama 1–4 lists the 50 sāhams, including "विवाह ३२", "पर स्त्री २८" and "कन्या ४९". **No Punarvivāha sāham appears.** — [TN (Jha)](https://archive.org/details/nxfu-tajika-neelakanthi-gudhartha-prakashika-commentary)
- **[PRIMARY] Related sāham (Paradāra / Anya-dāra, "other woman"):** TN 16: "सितादपास्यार्कमथान्यदाराह्वं सदा". The bhāṣya: "सर्वदा शुक्र में सूर्य को घटाकर … अन्यदारा (परस्त्री) सहम". **Formula:** Paradāra = Venus − Sun + Asc, always, with the same 30° proviso. Computability: C. TN treats this as an extra-marital-relationship sāham, not a second-marriage sāham. — [TN (Jha)](https://archive.org/details/nxfu-tajika-neelakanthi-gudhartha-prakashika-commentary)

**Activation of the Vivāha Sāham in the year**
- **[PRIMARY]** TN Sāhama 39: "विवाहसद्माधिपसौम्यदृष्टं युतं शुभैर्मूथशिलं शुभाप्तिम् । कुर्याद्यदा मिश्रसमेतदृष्टं कष्टादथ क्रूरमृतीश्वरैर्न ॥३९॥" Bhāṣya: if the Vivāha Sāham is joined or aspected by its own lord and benefics and forms an Ithasāla (मूथशिल), marriage is obtained. With mixed planets it comes with difficulty. If it is joined or aspected by malefics or the **8th lord**, there is no marriage. Inputs: sāham sign, its lord, Tājika aspects, Ithasāla. Computability: C for the aspect and Ithasāla tests. — [TN (Jha)](https://archive.org/details/nxfu-tajika-neelakanthi-gudhartha-prakashika-commentary)

**Varṣa 7th-bhāva marriage rules (TN Bhāvaphala, 7th house, Jha comm.)** — all [PRIMARY], [TN (Jha)](https://archive.org/details/nxfu-tajika-neelakanthi-gudhartha-prakashika-commentary)
- v.1: "बली सितोऽब्दाधिपतिः स्मरस्थः स्त्रीपक्षतः सौख्यकरो…" Strong Venus as year lord, in the 7th, gives happiness from the wife's side. Very high happiness if Jupiter aspects. Mutual love if Mars aspects as an adhikārī.
- v.2: With Venus in the varṣa 7th, a Mercury aspect gives an affair with a younger woman and a Saturn aspect one with an older woman. "गुरुदृष्ट्या नवा भार्या सन्ततिस्त्वरितस्ततः": a Jupiter aspect gives a **new wife** and quick progeny.
- v.3: "जन्मलग्नाधिपेऽस्तस्थे दारसौख्यं बलान्विते । जन्मशुक्रर्क्षमस्तेऽब्दे स्त्रीलाभाय सितेऽब्दपे". If the strong natal Lagna lord is in the varṣa 7th, or the **natal Venus's sign is the varṣa 7th** and Venus is the year lord, the person gains a wife.
- Commentary quote (विवाहयोग) attached to v.3: "शुक्रो जन्मनि यद्राशौ वर्षे लग्नेश्वरो यदि । तद्राशौ सप्तमस्थेऽपि पुंसः परिणयस्तदा". If the varṣa Lagna lord is in, or the varṣa 7th falls in, the sign natal Venus occupied, marriage (*pariṇaya*) takes place. Computability: C.
- v.4: "लग्नास्तनाथयोरित्थशाले स्त्रीलाभमादिशेत्". **Ithasāla between the varṣa Lagna lord and 7th lord gives gain of a wife.** If the wife-sāham lord or the 7th-bhāva lord is *vinaṣṭa* (combust, conjoined or aspected by malefics), there is trouble for the wife.
- v.6: if natal Jupiter sits in the natal-Venus sign in the varṣa and is in a kendra or trikoṇa, or the lord of the varṣa-lagna Hadda or the **Vivāha Sāham lord** sits in the natal-Venus sign in a kendra or trikoṇa, there is happiness from a wife.
- v.7: "इन्थिहाधिकृतस्थाने गुरुदृष्टा विवाहकृत्". Muntha in the place of one of the five adhikārīs and aspected by Jupiter **causes marriage**.
- v.8: Muntha in the 7th with Mars and the Sun, and the wife-sāham afflicted, gives trouble from wife and children.
- v.9–12: the natal 7th lord, or Venus, strong in the varṣa 7th and in Ithasāla with the Lagna lord, gives gain of a wife "ध्रुवम्". Mars as year lord aspected by Venus, or Venus as year lord aspected by Mars, or the dāra-sāham aspected by both, gives gain of a woman. "स्वामिदृष्टं स्त्रीसहमं शुक्रदृष्टं विवाहकृत्": a wife-sāham aspected by its lord and by Venus causes marriage.
- Raman, *Varshaphal* §92 (7th bhāva), translation: "He gets a wife (if not yet married) if annual lord remains in the 7th house which should be birth ascendant… pleasures, gain of wealth from wife etc are possible if the lords of the lagna and the seventh house enter into Iddasalayoga." — [Raman](https://archive.org/details/VarshaphalOrTheHinduProgressedHoroscope)

**Muntha**
- **[PRIMARY]** Muntha = (natal Lagna sign number + completed years) mod 12. Raman: "Add the number of the ascendant at the birth to the number of the years elapsed… Divide the sum by 12 … remainder represents the number of the sign of the Muntha." Computability: C. — [Raman §45](https://archive.org/details/VarshaphalOrTheHinduProgressedHoroscope)
- **[PRIMARY]** TN on Muntha in the 7th (Muntha-phala v.9): "कलत्र-बन्धु-व्यसनारिभीतिः… द्यूनोपगा चेन्मुथहा". With Muntha in the 7th: distress to wife and kin, fear of enemies, loss of wealth and dharma, disease. Raman agrees: "seventh — sickness quarrels and failure", and "Muntha in house seven aspected by the Sun makes him uneasy." So **Muntha in the 7th is not a classical marriage indicator.** The marriage-causing Muntha rule in TN is the Jupiter-aspected Muntha in an adhikārī's place (v.7 above). — [TN (Jha)](https://archive.org/details/nxfu-tajika-neelakanthi-gudhartha-prakashika-commentary); [Raman §56](https://archive.org/details/VarshaphalOrTheHinduProgressedHoroscope)

**Year lord (Varṣeśa)**
- Raman: the year lord is chosen from the five office-bearers (*pañcādhikārī*). Of these, the Sun-sign lord counts by day and the Moon-sign lord by night. Computability: C. — [Raman §45](https://archive.org/details/VarshaphalOrTheHinduProgressedHoroscope)

**In-year daśās**
- **Pātyāṁśa daśā (Patyayini)** [PRIMARY]: TN Daśā verses 1–3 and Raman §49 describe it. Drop the signs and sort the planets and Lagna by degrees within the sign (*kṛśāṁśa*). Each pātyāṁśa is the difference from the previous one, and the first equals its own degrees. The pātyāṁśas sum to the largest degree value. Each period = year length × pātyāṁśa ÷ that sum, with antardaśās in the same proportion. The first daśā goes to the planet with the smallest degree. Computability: C. — [TN (Jha)](https://archive.org/details/nxfu-tajika-neelakanthi-gudhartha-prakashika-commentary); [Raman §49](https://archive.org/details/VarshaphalOrTheHinduProgressedHoroscope)
- Raman, Śukra-daśā results: "If weak — quarrels with wife … Kanya — quarrels with wife or husband and separation". — [Raman §104](https://archive.org/details/VarshaphalOrTheHinduProgressedHoroscope)
- **Mudda daśā** [PRIMARY but marked *kṣepaka* (interpolation) in the Jha edition]: "जन्मर्क्षसंख्यासहिता गतान्दा द्व्यूनिता नन्दहृतावशेषात्". Take (janma-nakṣatra number + completed years − 2) mod 9. Remainder 1… gives the Mudda daśā lord in the order Sun, Moon, Mars, Rahu, Jupiter, Saturn, Mercury, Ketu, Venus (the Vimśottari order compressed into the year). Computability: C. — [TN (Jha)](https://archive.org/details/nxfu-tajika-neelakanthi-gudhartha-prakashika-commentary)

## Q3. Second-marriage timing specifics in either school

### Takeaway
Neither school's primary text examined here gives a standalone "second-marriage daśā" rule.
- Jaimini gives **plurality** conditions on the 2nd from UL (exalted planet or navāṁśa, *yugma*) and **spouse-loss timing** (Guru-Śūla daśās, 1/5/9 from Jupiter; 2nd-from-UL debility "in that evil period"). Practitioners extend this through Venus and Ketu on the A7/UL axis.
- Tajika has no Punarvivāha sāham in TN. The closest indicators are: Jupiter aspecting Venus in the varṣa 7th ("नवा भार्या", a new wife); the Vivāha-sāham activation rules applied again in a later year; and the Paradāra sāham (Venus − Sun + Asc) for other relationships.

### Cited Findings
- Jaimini Su. 6–8 (plurality and no wife in later life) and Su. 10–11 (Guru-Śūla daśā for wife's death) — [B.S. Rao](https://archive.org/details/Jaiminisutras1955EditionByBSRao)
- BPHS (Santhanam): Taurus as UL gives plurality (commentary), and the "THREE MARRIAGES" ślokas 40–41 give fixed ages — local BPHS OCR, lines ~22205 and ~10732.
- Rath: Venus/Ketu on the A7/UL axis gives two spouses, or "two marriages after losing a spouse"; Rahu or debilitated planets in the 2nd from UL plus a D-20 Nārāyaṇa daśā linked to the 8th/A8 time the break — [srath.com Darapada and Upapada](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/darapada-and-upapada/); [Rath, Narayana Dasa](https://scienceoflight.net/wp-content/uploads/extra/Narayana%20Dasa%20by%20Sanjay%20Rath.pdf)
- A Rath forum post (secondary copy) says the second house from UL indicates when separation becomes known, through divorce or death — [Course Hero copy](https://www.coursehero.com/file/97834394/docdownloadercom-pdf-marriage-sanjay-rath-dd-9ef900344af39e046aa73aa2d2a5517fpdf/)
- TN 7th-bhāva v.2: "गुरुदृष्ट्या नवा भार्या" — [TN (Jha)](https://archive.org/details/nxfu-tajika-neelakanthi-gudhartha-prakashika-commentary)

### Inferences
- A mechanical second-marriage data set could include:
  - 2nd-from-UL tests (exalted occupant or navāṁśa; *yugma*; AK or own lord present)
  - Guru-Śūla signs (1/5/9 from Jupiter) as spouse-loss windows
  - the 8th-from-UL / next-UL convention, if one is adopted (see Gaps)
  - Tajika: Vivāha-sāham activation in a year after a recorded first marriage
- These remain DATA. Whether a given window is a second marriage is the AI's call.
