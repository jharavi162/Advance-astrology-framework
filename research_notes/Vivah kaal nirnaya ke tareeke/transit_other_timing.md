## Q1. What are the exact conditions, orbs and sign-vs-degree definitions used?

### Takeaway
Classical texts time marriage by **sign/trine transit** (Jupiter, Venus or 7th lord passing a sign
trine to the Rasi or Navamsa of the lagna lord / 7th lord; lagna lord entering the 7th), **inside the
dasha** of a 7th-house planet; Ashtakavarga refines this to the **sign with more bindus** and to a
**3°45′ kakshya** (1/8 sign) whose lord contributed the bindu. Orbs are never degree-based in the
classical texts; degree-to-degree and "within ~a year Saturn then Jupiter" refinements are modern.
Muhurta conditions (Guru-bala from the natal Moon, eleven vivaha nakshatras, Chandra-bala) are for
**fixing a wedding date**, not predicting the year.

### Cited Findings

**(A) Classical natal-timing rules (Phaladeepika ch. X, Kalatra-bhava) — sign/trine based**
- PD X-12 (second half): "The marriage may be expected to come off when Venus or the lord of the 7th
  house in his orbit passes through a Sign which is triangular to the Rasi or Navamsa occupied by the
  Lord of the Lagna." Inputs: natal sign + D9 sign of lagna lord; transit sign of Venus/7th lord.
  Definition = whole-sign trine (1/5/9), no orb. — [PD X-12, local OCR l.7493-7497](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/Phaladeepika2ndEd.1950ByVSubrahmanyaSastri.txt)
- PD X-12 (first half, same verse): the wife's Janma Rasi is a sign trine to "the aforesaid Rasi or
  Navamsa", or the exaltation/debilitation sign of lords of 1st and 7th, or "that Rasi which contains
  the largest number of benefic dots in the Moon's Ashtakavarga table of the husband" (spouse
  description, not timing). — [PD X-12, l.7480-7486](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/Phaladeepika2ndEd.1950ByVSubrahmanyaSastri.txt)
- PD X-13: "The acquisition of a wife may happen during the Dasa period of the planet (1) posited in
  the 7th house, (2) aspecting the 7th house or (3) owning the 7th house. The same may also happen,
  when the lord of the Lagna in his orbit comes to the Rasi representing the 7th house." — [PD X-13, l.7500-7505](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/Phaladeepika2ndEd.1950ByVSubrahmanyaSastri.txt)
- PD X-14 (dasha + Jupiter transit combined; this is the closest classical analogue of a "Jupiter
  trigger"): "Find which of the two in the following two pairs is stronger: (1) the lords of the Rasi
  and Navamsa occupied by the lord of the 7th house; (2) Venus and the Moon. During the Dasa-period of
  that planet when Jupiter passes through a sign triangular to the Rasi or Navamsa occupied by the
  lord of the 7th, the marriage may be declared to take place." Inputs: 7th lord's natal Rasi + D9
  sign, their dispositors, Venus/Moon strength, Vimshottari dasha, transit Jupiter sign. — [PD X-14, l.7515-7523](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/Phaladeepika2ndEd.1950ByVSubrahmanyaSastri.txt)
- PD index entry confirms the topic heading "Time of marriage guessed through [Jupiter]'s transit. X-14". — [PD index, l.28603](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/Phaladeepika2ndEd.1950ByVSubrahmanyaSastri.txt)

**(B) Classical age-yogas (BPHS ch. "Effects of the Seventh House") — fixed ages, not transit**
- BPHS 7th-house chapter sl. 23-34 give marriage ages from yogas, e.g. sl. 26 "marry at 12 or 19 if
  Venus is in an angle from the ascendant while Saturn is in the 7th counted from Venus"; sl. 31
  "Should the 7th lord be in the 12th while the natal ascendant lord is in the 7th in Navamsha,
  marriage will be in 23rd/26th year"; sl. 32 "25th or 33rd year ... if the 8th lord is in the 7th as
  Venus is in Navamsha ascendant". Translator's note: these ages "will not be practical in all cases
  in modern social conditions ... will simply help in knowing of early or belated marriages." — [BPHS 7th-house ch., l.10600-10690 (book pp.125-126)](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/brihatparasarahorashastrabyr.santhanam.txt)

**(C) Gochara of Jupiter / Venus from the natal Moon (Phaladeepika ch. XXVI) — sign based, with Vedha**
- PD XXVI-2: Jupiter gives good results in transit in "the 7th, 9th, 2nd and 5th" from the Moon, and
  "all planets in the 11th"; "Venus in all places other than the 10th, 7th and 6th". — [PD XXVI-2, l.16165-16172](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/Phaladeepika2ndEd.1950ByVSubrahmanyaSastri.txt)
- PD XXVI-7 (Jupiter vedha pairs): "During his transit in the 2nd, 11th, 9th, 5th and 7th, Jupiter is
  good if the corresponding Vedha places, viz., 12th, 8th, 10th, 4th and 3rd are void of planets." — [PD XXVI-7, l.16258-16262](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/Phaladeepika2ndEd.1950ByVSubrahmanyaSastri.txt)
- PD XXVI-8 (Venus vedha): Venus is good in 1,2,3,4,5,8,9,12,11 unless marred by planets in the vedha
  places 8,7,1,10,9,5,11,6,3 respectively. — [PD XXVI-8, l.16275-16281](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/Phaladeepika2ndEd.1950ByVSubrahmanyaSastri.txt)
- PD XXVI (later, on Vedha/Latta): "A marriage celebrated during a Vedha will not be happy". — [PD XXVI, l.17869-17871](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/Phaladeepika2ndEd.1950ByVSubrahmanyaSastri.txt)

**(D) Ashtakavarga transit timing (BPHS ch. 70, 72; PD ch. XXIII-XXIV)**
- BPHS ch.70 sl.34-36 (Venus's AV): "There will be gain of wealth, land and happiness, and marriage,
  whenever Venus passes in transit through the rashis which have larger number of rekhas in the
  Ashtaka Varga of Venus. These gains will be from the directions of the 7th rashi from Venus and of
  its Trikona rashis. The effects should be judged ... after multiplying the rekhas in the 7th house
  from Venus by the Yoga Pinda." — [BPHS 70.34-36, l.77226-77232 (p.723)](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/brihatparasarahorashastrabyr.santhanam.txt)
- BPHS ch.70 illustration (Shodhya/Yoga pinda for spouse): rekhas in 7th from Venus × Yoga Pinda;
  ÷27 → nakshatra, ÷12 → rashi; "The wife will be in distress during the transit of Saturn through"
  that nakshatra and its trikona nakshatras and that rashi and its trikona rashis (worked example:
  3 × 72 = 216 → Revati / Pisces). NB: this pinda rule is given for **affliction to the wife**, not for
  the marriage event. — [BPHS 70 illus., l.77340-77352 (pp.724-725)](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/brihatparasarahorashastrabyr.santhanam.txt)
- BPHS ch.70 sl.19-20 (Sun's AV, applied to marriage muhurta): "No auspicious functions like marriage
  etc., should be performed in the month of rashis (that is, when the Sun transits that rashi) which
  has more number of dots in the Sun's Ashtaka Varga. The same applies to the Samvatsar of that rashi
  [that is, when the mean Jupiter transits that rashi]. Auspicious functions should be performed when
  the Sun or the mean Jupiter transits the rashi which has more rekhas in the Sun's Ashtaka Varga."
  (Here "dots" = malefic, "rekhas" = benefic in this translation's convention.) — [BPHS 70.19-20, l.76644-76649](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/brihatparasarahorashastrabyr.santhanam.txt)
- BPHS ch.72 sl.3-5 (Sarvashtakavarga): rashi with >30 rekhas favourable, 25-30 medium, <25 adverse;
  "Auspicious functions like marriage etc. should be performed when the planet on whose basis the time
  and date of functions are performed, moves into the rashi with favourable effects" (e.g. the Moon for
  Chandra-bala). — [BPHS 72.3-5, l.77782-77796](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/brihatparasarahorashastrabyr.santhanam.txt)
- BPHS ch.72 sl.30-31: "the auspiciousness of Ashtaka Varga should be got checked before performing any
  function, like marriage ... If a Rashi is not auspicious in Ashtaka Varga, then its auspiciousness
  should be checked from transit effects ... the auspiciousness of the Rashi in the Ashtaka Varga should
  be considered, as paramount." — [BPHS 72.30-31, l.78126-78133](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/brihatparasarahorashastrabyr.santhanam.txt)
- PD XXIII-14/15 (karaka-based bhava from AV, domain-general): count the AV of the karaka planet with
  the karaka's own sign as lagna; when any planet transits the house with the highest bindus "it
  advances that Bhava"; several planets transiting a high-bindu bhava together promote it "in an intense
  manner". For marriage this would be the 7th from Venus in Venus's AV (inference — PD states it
  generally, not for marriage). — [PD XXIII-14,15, l.15088-15108](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/Phaladeepika2ndEd.1950ByVSubrahmanyaSastri.txt)
- PD XXIII-16/18/19 (Kakshya / Prastara timing): "to definitely fix the exact time of its (benefic
  dot's) fruition, a Rasi is divided into 8 equal divisions and the divisions are allotted to the
  planets and the Lagna ... the effect will occur in the transit over the particular division owned by
  the planet that put forth the benefic dot." Order (sl.19): 1st Saturn, 2nd Jupiter, then Mars, Sun,
  Venus, Mercury, Moon, last = Lagna "according to their orbits". Sl.17 describes the 96-square
  Prastara table (8 rows × 12 signs). Each kakshya = 30°/8 = 3°45′. — [PD XXIII-16..19, l.15116-15180](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/Phaladeepika2ndEd.1950ByVSubrahmanyaSastri.txt)
- PD XXIII definition of Shodhyapinda: "After the Trikona and Ekadhipatya reductions have been made in
  any Ashtakavarga, the sum-total of all the remaining figures is called the (Sodhyapinda)". PD XXIV-12
  (Venus AV) uses the sign with most bindus for the wife's direction/birth sign, not timing. PD XXIV
  pinda×bindus÷27/÷12 → star/sign rules are given for father, mother, native's demise, etc. — no
  marriage-event pinda rule was found in PD. — [PD XXIII/XXIV, l.15293-15317, 15394-15402](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/Phaladeepika2ndEd.1950ByVSubrahmanyaSastri.txt)

**(E) Muhurta conditions for fixing the wedding date (MC)**
- Guru-bala (Vasishtha via MC Piyushadhara): Jupiter (gochara from Janma-rashi) in the 3rd (bandhu
  reading uncertain in OCR), 6th (ripu) and 10th requires pūjā (remedial worship); "nechchhanti pūjāṁ
  janige vyayasthe purātanā aṣṭamage'pi rāśau" — the ancients do not accept even pūjā when Jupiter is
  in the 1st (janma), 12th or 8th. 4th/janma placement handled "by regional custom"; if a qualified
  groom is found and the girl's age is passing, marriage may be done even without Guru-shuddhi with
  "double/triple" pūjā (Varaha quoted: "kuryād gurau śuddhivivarjite'pi varasya lābhe śubhadaṁ
  vivāhaṁ vadanti garga-cyavanādimukhyāḥ"). — [MC Sanskara-prakarana commentary, l.20060-20066](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/mc.txt)
- Garga (in MC): "mauñjībandhe vivāhe ca pratiṣṭhāyāṁ ... gocareṇaiva kartavyaṁ vedhādikam akāraṇam" —
  Guru-bala for upanayana, marriage, pratishtha is by **gochara only; vedha is irrelevant**. Contrary
  view (Rajamartanda): where gochara-bala is lacking, use **Ashtakavarga-bala**. — [MC l.20073-20078](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/mc.txt)
- MC dvirāgamana commentary (Chandeshvara): if a girl's menarche occurs in her father's house, absence
  of Sun/Guru-shuddhi is not a fault for her journey to the husband's home, given good lagna/tithi and
  Chandra-Tara shuddhi. — [MC l.21366-21373](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/mc.txt)
- The MC table of contents lists, in Vivaha-prakarana (ch. 6): "kanyā-vivāhakāla, guru-śuddhi,
  vivāhocita māsa, janmamāsādi niṣedha, jyeṣṭhamāsādi vicāra ... nirvedha-nakṣatrāṇi ... gaṇa-maitra,
  bhakūṭa, nāḍī ... 18 se ūpar guṇaikya", lagna-doṣas, "vivāha nakṣatra-vedha ... pañcaśalākā cakra",
  godhūli-kāla. — [MC contents, l.595-624, 2617-2985; l.19586](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/mc.txt)
- Vivaha nakshatra list (secondary; verse text not in the local OCR): eleven nakshatras — Rohini,
  Mrigashira, Magha, Uttara Phalguni, Hasta, Swati, Anuradha, Mula, Uttara Ashadha, Uttara Bhadrapada,
  Revati — attributed to MC and B.V. Raman's *Muhurtha*; must be **nirvedha** (free of vedha, checked via
  Pañcaśalākā/Sapta-śalākā chakra). — [drikpanchang (search summary)](https://www.drikpanchang.com/panchang/nakshatra/auspicious-marriage-nakshatra.html); [dekhopanchang vivah muhurta (search summary)](https://dekhopanchang.com/en/learn/vivah-muhurta)
- Common folk maxim (not located in MC OCR): for a bride Guru-bala, for a groom Surya-bala, for both
  Chandra-bala. — not verified (see Gaps).

**(F) Double transit — modern (B.V. Raman / K.N. Rao) — sign + Parashari aspects**
- Rule as commonly taught: marriage dasha (7th lord, planet in 7th, or Venus) **plus** transit Saturn
  **and** Jupiter both aspecting/occupying the 7th house or 7th lord "simultaneously (within a period of
  6-12 months)"; Jupiter brings opportunity, Saturn "commitment, formalization". Some sites credit the
  principle to **B.V. Raman** rather than Rao; aspect sets differ between teachers (one allows 1/3/5/9/11,
  another uses only the special 3/7/10 for Saturn and 5/7/9 for Jupiter). — [dekhopanchang module 11-3 (search summary)](https://dekhopanchang.com/en/learn/modules/11-3); [onlinejyotish lesson 151 (search summary)](https://www.onlinejyotish.com/free-astrology/astrology-lessons/lesson_151.php)
- Variant (secondary): double transit over the 7th house or lord, lagna or lord, or natal Moon; dasha
  also checked from the Moon chart; marriage materialises when Saturn's influence comes first and
  Jupiter's follows within about a year. — [Jyotishpermishuri (search summary)](https://www.jyotishpermishuri.com/transit-effects-of-slow-moving-planets-and-faster-moving-planets-for-the-timing-of-marriage/)
- Rao's published double-transit book (Exotic India listing) is framed for **career** ("Ups and Downs in
  Career ... using transits of Saturn and Jupiter"), tested "in K.N. Rao's astrological laboratories". — [Exotic India listing](https://exoticindia.com/book/details/ups-and-downs-in-career-replicable-astrological-techniques-using-transits-of-saturn-and-jupiter-nae715)
- Critique: summary of the theory as "marriage chances are high when transit Saturn and Jupiter aspect
  natal 1/7 axis or their natal lords"; critic notes Jupiter's four sign-contacts (1,5,7,9) hit the 7th
  or its lord ~1/3 of the time, so the test has low discrimination. — [IndiaDivine thread (search summary)](https://www.indiadivine.org/content/topic/1514904-why-shri-kn-raos-double-transit-doesnt-make-sense/)

**(G) BNN / Nadi (R.G. Rao lineage) — sign/star contact, degree as refinement**
- Rao's karaka list: "(4) Venus for marriages"; timer = Jupiter (jeeva) transit, ~1 sign/yr, 12-yr
  rounds; read karaka + adjacent planets + 7th from karaka + 12th + sign-lord + exchanges. Examples from
  the book: "Jupiter … contacts Venus in Virgo … around the native's 30th year, marriage is likely to
  take place"; "when Jupiter transits the star occupied by Venus at birth …"; "entry into Libra owned by
  Venus, indicate that marriage …"; Saturn: "When Saturn transits Capricorn his conjunction with Venus
  will bring about the marriage". Female chart: husband additionally read from Mars. — [repo docs/knowledge/bnn/01_timing_method.md (quotes R.G. Rao BNN)](/home/user/Advance-astrology-framework/docs/knowledge/bnn/01_timing_method.md); [docs/BNN_METHODS.md §8](/home/user/Advance-astrology-framework/docs/BNN_METHODS.md)
- "Contact" = same sign or 7th from (and, in the course notes, trine/2nd/12th "golden relations");
  "degree-to-degree / star-level matching sharpens it ... not the primary gate". Jupiter aspecting/over
  Venus → early marriage; Saturn on Venus → delay; "double approval" = Jupiter and Saturn both contacting
  the karaka. (Double-approval framing is the repo's synthesis, not a quoted Rao verse.) — [01_timing_method.md §4,§6](/home/user/Advance-astrology-framework/docs/knowledge/bnn/01_timing_method.md)

**(H) Bhrigu Bindu — modern, secondary only**
- Definition: midpoint of natal Moon and Rahu, taken on the **shorter arc**; Jupiter crossing it is
  described as a positive window (marriage, childbirth cited), Saturn crossing as karmic difficulty;
  one source cautions not to predict from a single transit without dasha support. — [biplov.dev](https://biplov.dev/en/speciallagnas/bhrigubindu); [astroindus](https://astroindus.com/bhrigu-nandi-nadi/bhrigu-bindu/)

**(I) Sudarshana Chakra dasha — classical framework, no marriage-specific rule**
- BPHS ch.74 sl.1-6: three concentric charts from Lagna, Moon and Sun. Sl.21-23: each house from the 1st
  gets a 1-year dasha (year n → house n as "Ascendant of the year", cycle repeats every 12 years), each
  house a 1-month antardasha, pratyantar 2½ days, vidasha 12½ ghatikas; computed by **solar months**.
  Sl.24-26: benefics in 1,4,7,10,5,9,8 from the year-house → good; malefics in 3,6,11 good; Rahu/Ketu-only
  houses harmful. So the year in which the 7th house (from L/Mo/Su) is the year-lagna is year 7, 19, 31,
  43... (inference: years 7+12k; the 7th from the year-lagna is examined in every year). — [BPHS 74, l.78487-78540, 79530-79600](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/brihatparasarahorashastrabyr.santhanam.txt)

**(J) Yogini dasha — secondary**
- Used as a parallel clock to Vimshottari: 7th lord's MD/AD/PD are primary windows; agreement of
  Vimshottari and Yogini raises confidence; one forum calls it dependable when birth time is uncertain. — [anvayajyotish (search summary)](https://anvayajyotish.com/blog/marriage-timing-prediction-vedic-astrology); [dkscore Yogini+Varshaphala (search summary)](https://www.dkscore.com/jyotishmedium/marriage-timing-with-yogini-dasha-and-varshaphala-a-step-by-step-vedic-astrology-guide-2261)

**(K) Navamsha — classical uses inside timing**
- Classical: D9 sign of lagna lord (PD X-12) and D9 sign of 7th lord + its dispositor (PD X-14) are the
  reference points for Jupiter/Venus/7th-lord trine transits; BPHS 7th-house sl.31-32 use lagna-lord in
  7th of D9 and Venus in D9 lagna for marriage age. — [PD X-12,14](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/Phaladeepika2ndEd.1950ByVSubrahmanyaSastri.txt); [BPHS 7th-house ch.](/tmp/claude-0/s/txt/brihatparasarahorashastrabyr.santhanam.txt)
- Secondary (Sanjay Rath-attributed): 7th as 11th from 9th = "gains of dharma"; D9 read through the 9th. — [Scribd "Marriage – Sanjay Rath" (search summary)](https://www.scribd.com/document/265405739/Marriage-Sanjay-Rath)

### Inferences
- Computability: everything in (A)–(E), (G)–(I) is fully deterministic from a sidereal chart + transit
  ephemeris: sign trines (PD X-12/14), dasha lords (PD X-13/14), Jupiter/Venus gochara from Moon with vedha
  (PD XXVI), BAV/SAV bindu counts in transit sign, kakshya index = floor(longitude_in_sign / 3.75°) with the
  PD order Sa, Ju, Ma, Su, Ve, Me, Mo, Lagna, Shodhya pinda, Sudarshana year-house = ((age_years) mod 12)+1,
  Bhrigu Bindu = shorter-arc midpoint. Interpretation (which reference point, how many confirmations) is
  judgment.
- PD X-14 is the strongest **classical** text-anchor for "Jupiter transit triggers marriage within a dasha";
  the double-transit (Jupiter+Saturn both) is **not found** in PD/BPHS and is a 20th-century practitioner rule.
- The classical AV marriage rule (BPHS 70.34-36) is about **Venus's** transit through high-bindu signs of
  its own BAV, not Jupiter/Saturn bindus; Jupiter/Saturn AV transit rules for marriage are modern extensions
  of the PD XXIII-14 general principle.
- Classical definitions are whole-sign; "orb" only enters via kakshya (3°45′ slice) or modern degree locks.

## Q2. Which rules are classical (text + verse) vs modern practitioner?

### Takeaway
Classical (verse-cited): PD X-12/13/14, PD XXVI-2/7/8 + vedha note, PD XXIII-14..19 (kakshya/prastara),
BPHS 7th-house sl.23-34 (ages), BPHS 70.19-20 & 34-36, BPHS 72.3-5 & 30-31, BPHS 74 (Sudarshana), MC
guru-bala/vedha commentary (Vasishtha, Garga, Varaha, Rajamartanda, Chandeshvara). Modern: Jupiter–Saturn
double transit (B.V. Raman / K.N. Rao), BNN Jupiter-over-Venus/Mars (R.G. Rao's BNN edition — Nadi but
published 20th c.), Bhrigu Bindu, Yogini-for-marriage usage, UL transit (Sanjay Rath school).
