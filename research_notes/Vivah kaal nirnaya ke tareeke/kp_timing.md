## Q1. KSK's step-by-step procedure to fix the date (Reader IV worked examples) — significators, order of strength, ruling planets, transit

### Takeaway
KSK's recipe is: (1) promise from the 7th-cusp sub-lord (signifies 2/7/11 → promised; 1/6/10 or 4/6/10 → denied); (2) list significators of 2, 7, 11 in a fixed 4/5-level strength order (planets in the star of occupants > occupants > planets in star of owners > owners > conjoined/aspected); (3) filter each significator by its own sub-lord (must connect to 2/7/11, not 1/6/10/12) and by retrogression; (4) intersect with the **Ruling Planets (RP)** at the moment of judgment (day lord, Moon star-lord, Moon sign-lord, Lagna sign-lord, Lagna star-lord, + nodes as agents); (5) pick the Dasa–Bhukti–Antara(–Sookshma) of the surviving planets; (6) pin the day by the **Sun's** transit (month/day) — sometimes Jupiter (year), Moon (days), or the Dasa lord — through the sign/star/sub of the significators/RP. All steps are mechanical except the "delay" judgement (which bhukti a malefic aspect holds back) and the final choice among tied RP.

### Cited Findings

**A. Promise filter — 7th cusp sub-lord**
- "Note the sub-lord of the 7th cusp. If it is a significator of house 7 or 2 or 11, marriage is promised Rahu, the sub lord of the 7th cusp, is in the 7th house itself. Hence marriage will take place." — [R4 p.122](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers). Inputs: Placidus 7th cusp longitude → sign/star/sub lords; house-significations of that sub-lord. **Computable.**
- Denial: "According to Krishnamurti Padhdhati a person is denied of marriage when the 7th Cusp sub lord is deposited in tbe constellation of the significators of 4, 6 and 10 because the houses 2, 7 and 11 show the marriage, and hence the 12th house to these houses indicates denial of the marriage." and "The simple rule is, note the sublord of the 7th Cusp; if the sub lord is connected with 4, 6, 10th houses, one will be denied of marriage." … "it is the sub lord which is mainly responsible to give clear idea" — [R4 pp.106–107](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers). **Computable.**
- Significator's own sub as gate: "Further the sub lord of each of the significators. If a planet is in such a position that its sub lord is connected with the houses 2, 7 and 11 then this planet is useful. But if the sub is lord of 1, 6, 10 and is the significator of 12 houses, marriage cannot take place." — [R4 p.119](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers). **Computable.**
- 6th-house veto **[case, author unclear]**: "if a planet happens to be the significator of any of the houses 2, 7 and 11 and it itself happens to be the strong and single significator of the sixth house, it will not give the marriage … as the sixth house is separation from wife" — [R4 p.146](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers). **Computable** (needs a "single/strong significator" definition).

**B. Significator strength order (2, 7, 11)**
- KSK's dialogue answer to "give out the order of judging the significators": "(1) Planets tenanted in the constellation of the occupants of the houses 2 or 7 or 11. (2) The occupants of 2 or 7 or 11. (3) Planets situated in the constellation of the owners of the signs where these cusps fall. (4) Owners. (5) Planets conjoined with or aspected by any of those found out in the previous four conditions." — [R4 p.119](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers). **Computable.**
- Same list restated in a later case (a)–(f): "(a) The planets in the constellation of the occupants of the houses 2, 7 or 11, (b) the occupants. (c) the planets in the constellation of the lords … (d) the lords of 2, 7 or 11, (e) planets conjoined with them, or (f) planets aspected by them" — [R4 pp.128–129](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers).
- Tenant-over-lord principle (Reader VI): "planets in the constellation of Jupiter, Rahu and Kethu will be strong significators … This does not mean that Rahu, Kethu and Jupiter cannot give … But the planets in their stars are Stronger to give the results. (Tenant stronger.)" — [R6 EPUB p.187 / printed p.179](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers).
- Node appropriation: "Though Saturn is a significator being lord of the 2nd house … its results are appropriated by Kethu who is posited in one of Saturn's Makara. Saturn is therefore left out." — [R4 p.123](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers).
- Houses 3 and 9 for *engagement* (Reader III Q&A): "The significators of the house 2, 7 and 11 denote marriage in their conjoined periods. But the planets which are the significators of 2 or 7 or 11 is also the significator of 3 and also 9 the marriage will be engaged in their conjoined periods. Because the third house shows the agreement of the native and the 9th … the consent and agreement of the other party." — [R3 EPUB p.431](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers). **Computable.**
- Exclusion of 6/8/12-linked significators **[case, author unclear]**: "Of these, Jupiter, Saturn and Mars do not occupy the star or sub of planets owning houses 6, 8 or 12 … we shall take Jupiter, Saturn, and Mars as the strongest significators." — [R4 p.126](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers).

**C. Ruling Planets at judgment = the filter ("fruitful significators")**
- "When majority of the planets are connected with the houses 2, 7 and 11, how to eliminate the useless ones and select the useful ones" → "Ruling planets at the moment of judgement must agree." — [R4 p.119](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers).
- "Now the question arises how to select the strongest and the fruitful ones. I have discovered that the ruling planets at the moment of judgment will be helpful and they are the real benefactors." — [R4 p.190](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers). (This is the closest KSK definition of "fruitful significators": significators of 2/7/11 that are also RP.)
- Definition of RP (Reader VI): day lord; "The lord of the star in which the Ascendant rises in the east. The lord of the sign rising in the east"; "The lord of the star in which Moon passes … The lord of the sign in which Moon is"; nodes in a sign of an RP join as agents; "Among these, these deposited in the constellation of a retrograde planet should be rejected. Those which are retrograde will cause delay till it is retrograde and they will be fruitful, after they in direct motion reach a particular sub." — [R6 EPUB p.131 / printed p.123](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers). **Computable** (needs only the judgment moment + place).
- "Planets which are in the sub of those who are detrimental to the matters of a particular Bhava, indicate when the matter will fall through. Those which are in direct motion and which are the significators of the matter under judgment, they give the desired results." — [R6 printed pp.123–124](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers).
- RP recur at fruition: "(a) the ruling planets at the time of query are the ruling planets at the time of birth and (b) the ruling planets at the time of fulfilment of the querist's desire are also the ruling planets at the moment of query." — [R4 p.132](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers). Also "The ruling planets at the time of query are the ruling planets at the time of fructification of the expected result." — [R4 p.205](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers) (reunion case, not marriage). And "One should never omit to note the ruling planets at the moment of judgement." — [R4 p.209](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers).
- Retrograde RP timing (Reader VI 2nd-marriage case): "Mars and Jupiter are themselves retrograde. But that does not prevent them from giving results. Only thing is that they are incapable of giving results as long as they are retrograde. Once they become direct they will give." … "Jupiter will become direct on 23-5-1969. So it can give result during 23-5-1969 and 18-2-1970." — [R6 EPUB p.175 / printed p.167](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers). **Computable** (station dates).

**D. Choosing the D-B-A and the delay rule**
- Delay by aspect (KSK case): "If the dasa is ruled by the planet 'A' and a malefic 'B' aspect 'A' then one cannot have success till 'A' Dasa 'B' Bhukti or 'A' Bhukti 'B' anthra is over: Later the benefic 'A' will give the favourable result during the sub-period of a favourable significator." … "Later a strong significator will give the marriage, in his sub-period (Bhukti)." — [R4 pp.127–128](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers). Mostly computable (aspect rule needs a fixed aspect definition); "how long" Saturn delays is partly judgement.
- Saturn–Moon delay: "Whenever Saturn has got any connection whatsoever with Moon, there will be some obstacle or impediment, not only during negotiation but also at the time of fixation and even at the time of celebration" — [R4 p.75](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers). Interpretive.
- Generic statement **[case, author unclear]**: "Marriage will take place during the conjoined periods of the significators for houses 2, 7 and 11, when transits are also in harmony." — [R4 p.125](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers).
- Cross-chart check (Reader III): "If either is running A dasa, B bhukti and C anthra, note whether A, B and C are the ruling planets at the moment of the birth of the other; or the planets deposited in the constellation of A or B or C will be the ruling planets at the moment of the birth of the partner. This is a certainty." — [R3 EPUB p.442](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers). Computable only if partner's birth data exist.

**E. Transit / day-fixing — which body to move**
- KSK's scaling rule (Reader VI): "If a matter is to be materialised in days and within a month you take the position of Moon … But if it would take more than a month, move the Sun from the present position of the Sun. If the matter is to fructify in some years move Jupiter … If one asks which month I can celebrate the marriage of my daughter in that year, Sun must reach the position governed by the Ruling planets." and "The Moon must reach the position governed by the ruling planets conjointly … Reject those ruling planets situated in the constellation of the retrograde planets" — [R6 EPUB p.139 / printed p.131](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers). **Computable** (search Sun's longitude for sign-lord/star-lord/sub-lord ∈ RP set).
- Transit doctrine (Reader V): "Sun who is to indicate the month of marriage was in Venus star Venus sub … Thus all planets were transiting in the constellation of the significators and sub of the significators. Let a planet transit in any sign, or in any house. As per KP you never bother about it. What you should find out is, the transit of the significators in the constellation and sub of the significators of a particular matter." — [R5 EPUB p.364 / printed pp.330–331](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers). In that case "Rahu Dasa gave marriage the moment it started. By transit Jupiter was in Rahu star Rahu sub." — [R5 printed p.330](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers). **Computable.**
- Sub as stage-marker (Reader III): "if Venus star Barani is transited by lord of 2 or 7 or lord of 11 in that year, then whenever the significator moves in favorable sub, negotiations regarding marriage will come nearer and whenever it transits unfavourable sub, result at that time will be delayed or one may reject the offer … the various events about marriage is clearly shown by the sub, transited by the significator." — [R3 EPUB p.499](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers). Also staged transits in R5: "Jupiter in Moon's sub, brings the negotiation nearer … When it transits in the sub of Jupiter which was in Gemini in 11, marriage is celebrated." — [R5](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers) (page not recovered).

### Inferences
- The engine-computable core is: cusps (Placidus) → sign/star/sub lords; 4/5-level significator table for 2/7/11 (and 3/9 for engagement); sub-lord gate per significator; RP set at a given moment (5 lords + node agents, minus planets in the star of a retrograde planet); Vimśottari D-B-A-S windows; and a forward search for dates when the Sun (or Moon/Jupiter/Dasa lord) occupies sign/star/sub ruled by the RP/D-B-A lords. All of this is DATA; the choice between tied candidates (e.g., "the latter is stronger"), "delay till which bhukti" and weekday selection are judgement for the AI.
- The repeated KSK pattern for the day is **Sun's sign-lord = bhukti (or dasa) lord, star-lord = antara lord, sub-lord = another significator**, inside the antara window — e.g. examples 7 and 8.
- KP is explicitly used here for *timing* by KSK, which contrasts with the repo guide's stance "KP is quality/yes-no (never timing)". That stance is a project design choice, not KSK's doctrine; worth noting for the report writer.

### Gaps
- The 14-7-1965 example (R4 p.75) shows no sub-lord/RP/transit derivation in the OCR — the exact-day step for it could not be reconstructed.
- KSK does not give a numeric weighting among the five levels; ties are settled ad hoc by RP and "stronger" judgement.
- The RP set is taken at the *moment of judgment* (horary-like), making the natal method non-reproducible without a fixed judgment time — an engine must take that time as an explicit input.
- Reader V page for the "Jupiter in Moon's sub brings the negotiation nearer" passage not recovered.

## Q2. Weight given to Rahu/Ketu as agents

### Takeaway
Nodes are treated as *stronger* than the planets they represent: they give first the results of planets they are conjoined with (then aspected by), then of their sign-lord; they are added to the RP when they occupy a sign of an RP; and a node in either sign of a dual-sign owner represents that owner fully. In several examples a node is preferred as the D-B-A lord/agent over the planet it represents.

### Cited Findings
- "The nodes having no sign of their own first offer the results of the planets with which they are conjoined and secondly of the lord of the sign in which they are placed. Kethu, thus, is Sani's agent and Rahu deputises for Moon." — [R4 p.122](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers).
- "I had said that Rahu or Kethu will give first of all the results indicated by the planets with which they are conjoined or by which they are aspected. Hence include the two nodes. Reject Sun, take Kethu." — [R4 p.190](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers).
- 7th-sub-lord as node: "If the sublord of the 7th cusp is either Rahu or Kethu … the results should be referred to that planet with which Rahu or Kethu is conjoined; if no planet is conjoined with Rahu or Kethu, then read the result of the planet which aspects Rahu or Kethu; if no planet is conjoined with and no planet aspects then take the lord of the [sign]" — [R4 p.98](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers).
- RP agents: "If Rahu or Kethu were to occupy a sign then Rahu and Kethu will act as an agent of the lord of the sign … If a planet owning two houses, rules this sign and either Rahu or Kethu were to be in either of the two signs, node must be included." — [R6 printed p.123](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers).
- "according to Krishnamurti if either of the nodes were to occupy either of the signs belonging to a planet, then the node will give the results of the lord of both the signs. So Rahu in Meena is more powerful than Jupiter" — [R4 p.209](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers).
- Node preferred over 7th-house occupant: "even though Moon is in the 7th Bhava, we have to give preference to Rahu and planets in its constellation." — [R4 p.149](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers).
- Node stronger than a plain RP: "Rahu is a node. It represents both Jupiter and Venus (i.e. lord of rasi, star and lagna in ruling planets). Therefore Rahu is stronger than Mars who is only day-lord in the ruling planets. Therefore Rahu anthra will give second marriage." — [R6 printed p.167](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers).
- Node as "turncoat": "Thus, Rahu cooperating with the dasa lord, Surya, gave marriage and in the dasa of Chandra it caused you bereavement" — [R4 p.181](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers).

### Inferences
- Computable rule set: node's represented planets = {conjunct planets} → else {aspecting planets} → plus sign-lord (+ both signs if sign-lord owns two). The aspect definition (Vedic vs. KSK's Western-style aspects) must be fixed by the engine; KSK's text mixes both.
- In ranking, a node that represents several RP outranks a single-role RP (R6 p.167) — a deterministic tie-break the engine could expose as data (count of RP roles represented), without converting it to a verdict.

### Gaps
- KSK does not specify an orb for "conjoined" nodes or which aspect system applies; examples vary.

## Q3. Timing a SECOND marriage / remarriage after divorce

### Takeaway
Promise: the 7th-cusp sub-lord must be Mercury, or in a dual sign, or in the star of a planet in/owning a dual sign (KSK in R4 also: "lord of the sub or the lord of the constellation in which the sub-lord is situated is in a dual sign or happens to be a dual planet"), AND (Reader VI) that sub-lord must signify 2 or 11. Additionally the **2nd-cusp sub-lord** connected to the 7th → second (legal) marriage; connected to 11 → liaison. Timing: significators of **2 and 11** (2nd house = second marriage), filtered by RP, in their D-B-A; divorce first via 1/6/10 (12 to 2/7/11) significators; day via the Dasa lord's or Sun's transit in the star/sub of RP.

### Cited Findings
- Reader VI houses: "houses 2, 7 and 11 are to be judged for first marriage. For second marriage, houses 2 and 11 are to be judged." — [R6 EPUB p.172 / printed p.164](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers).
- Reader VI two-condition rule: "Second marriage is indicated if the following two conditions are simultaneously satisfied: (i) The sub-lord of the 7th cusp is deposited in a dual sign or in the constellation of a planet in or owning a dual sign or if the sub-lord of the 7th cusp is Mercury; and (ii) the sub-lord of the 7th cusp is also a significator of either of the houses 2 or 11. If any one of the above two conditions is not satisfied, then second marriage is denied." — [R6 EPUB p.173 / printed p.165](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers). **Computable.**
- Reader VI full timing worked example (horary no. 29, Bombay, 6-5-1969): all nine planets signify 2 & 11 → "When many signify, the best course … is to go for Ruling planets" (Mars day, Jupiter rasi, Venus star, Venus lagna; Rahu+Ketu added as conjunct Venus/Jupiter); "In Venus Dasa, Jupiter Bhukti is running. Jupiter is lord of 11th. So you will have second marriage in Jupiter Bhukti … Jupiter will become direct on 23-5-1969" → Rahu antara chosen over Mars (see Q2) → "To pinpoint the exact date of marriage consider the transit of the dasa lord Venus. Venus transits Jupiter sub in Rahu star Swathi in the sign Tula owned by Venus on 13-11-1969. The day is Thursday owned by Jupiter. Moon transits Venus star Purvashada in Jupiter sign Dhanu on this day." — [R6 EPUB pp.173–175 / printed pp.165–167](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers). Note: here the **Dasa lord's** transit (not the Sun's) fixes the day, with weekday and Moon checked against RP.
- R4 KSK general rule: "As per K.P. if the sublord of 7th cusp is either Mercury or any planet occupying dual Sign, more than one wife." — [R4 p.85](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers).
- R4 KSK dialogue: "Note the sub-lord of the 7th cusp. If the lord of the sub or the lord of the constellation in which the sub-lord is situated is in a dual sign or happens to be a dual planet, then the second marriage is promised. Rahu is the lord of the sub of the 7th cusp; it is in Dhanus a dual sign. Hence two marriages are indicated. Further, Rahu is in the 2nd house which rules the second wife." — [R4 p.182](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers).
- R4 same case, timing (remarriage after widowhood): "This should come off in the dasa of Chandra which is in the constellation of Kethu acting as the agent of lord of 11 and sub of Sukra owning the 4th house; in the bhukti of Guru, lord of 2 occupying its own constellation and sub of Sani, a significator of 2nd house by occupying Rahu's constellation; and in the anthra of Sani. It will be between 5-4-1970 and 21-6-1970. As at the moment of judgment, Chandra is in Bharani star, when Surya transits in Bharani and sub of Chandra, i.e. around 1-5-1970 the second marriage will take place." — [R4 p.182](https://archive.org/details/kp-readers). This is the "Sun transits Bharani and sub of Chandra" rule: **Sun's transit through the judgment-moment Moon's star, in the sub of the Moon (the Dasa lord)**, inside the antara window. Computable given a judgment time. Preceding: "Chandra is in the constellation of Kethu in a dual sign and can give both the results. ### Inferences
- Two slightly different promise rules exist in KSK's own texts: R4 (dual sign/dual planet/Mercury connection of the 7th sub-lord or its star-lord) and R6 (same + must also signify 2 or 11). An engine should expose both booleans separately as DATA rather than merge them.
- Remarriage timing = first time a 1/6/10(/12) signification block (divorce/separation) ends, then the D-B-A of 2/11 significators filtered by RP; the date via Sun or the Dasa lord transiting star/sub of RP. All mechanical given a judgment moment.
- The 2nd-cusp-sub-lord → 7 (legal remarriage) vs → 11 (liaison) rule appears in a case of unclear authorship in R4 and should be labelled practitioner doctrine rather than KSK's direct wording.

### Gaps
- No KSK text found giving a separate rule for a **third** marriage beyond "cusp of 2 for second marriage and so on" (R4 p.87) — the "and so on" (presumably 9th cusp = 3rd from 7) is not spelled out in what was read.
- The repo task mentioned "2nd cusp sub-lord" as a KSK timing rule; in the texts read it appears only in the unclear-author case (R4 p.147) and in description/survival contexts (R4 p.87), not as a KSK timing procedure.
- Reader V and Reader III were searched only by keyword; there may be additional marriage-timing transit cases not captured.
