## Q1. Which cusp sub-lord rules marriage and remarriage (7th; any role for the 2nd, 9th and 11th)?

### Takeaway
All sources agree that the **7th cusp sub-lord (CSL)** is the main judge of marriage. Its significations of 2, 7 and 11 give the promise. For a *second* marriage, later practitioners add a check on the **2nd CSL**. The 2nd CSL linked to 7 points to a legal remarriage; linked only to 11, it points to companionship without marriage. A **9th CSL** test (the 9th is 3rd from 7th, "another spouse") exists, but it is fringe and openly assumption-based. One practitioner also uses the **11th CSL** to judge divorce itself.

### Cited Findings
- KP marriage promise: the 7th CSL signifying 2, 7 and 11 promises marriage. Signifying 6, 8 or 12 suggests obstruction (some sites). Another variant names 1, 6 and 10 as the obstacle houses. — [jagannathhora (2-7-11 formula)](https://jagannathhora.com/timing-marriage-2-7-11-formula/); [roxyapi KP guide](https://roxyapi.com/blogs/kp-astrology-krishnamurti-paddhati-sub-lord-horary-guide); [dekhopanchang KP](https://dekhopanchang.com/en/learn/kp-system)
- KSK is quoted (secondhand, no page reference) as saying: "For more than one marriage Prof. Krishnamurti has said that **the only rule to be followed is to note the sub-lord of the 7th cusp**." — [astrogle, Second marriage and Divorce](https://www.astrogle.com/astrology/second-marriage-divorce-in-astrology.html) (the same text is mirrored at [myastroconsult blogspot](http://myastroconsult.blogspot.com/2013/05/second-marriage-and-divorce-issues-in.html))
- Practitioner "Rule 2": "second marriage is indicated if the sub-lord of the 2nd cusp is also a significator of either of the houses 7 or 11." — [astrogle](https://www.astrogle.com/astrology/second-marriage-divorce-in-astrology.html), as summarised in a search snippet
- 2nd CSL refinement: "A strong connection between the sub lord of the 2nd cusp and the 7th house suggests a second marriage. An exclusive connection with the 11th house hints at non-marital companionship." Another summary gives the reason: the 7th rules legal bond, the 11th rules attachment or friendship. — [Scribd "2nd marriage divorce"](https://www.scribd.com/document/592038252/2nd-marriage-divorce); [Kanak Bhai KP second-marriage research (Scribd)](https://www.scribd.com/doc/241821607/Kp-Second-Marriage-Reserach-by-Kanak-Bhai)
- Jagannath Hora treats the **2nd CSL as the "primary indicator"** of a second marriage: "If the 2nd cusp Sub-Lord signifies houses 2, 7, and 11, second marriage is promised. If it signifies 6, 8, or 12, second marriage faces denial." — [jagannathhora, Second Marriage Predictions](https://jagannathhora.com/second-marriage-predictions-kp/)
- The 9th CSL rule ("Rule 3") rests on the assumption that the second spouse is a younger sibling of the first. Another source says: "As the 3rd from the 7th, the 9th can represent 'another' marriage in some traditions. This is less standard in KP but may be examined as supporting evidence." — [astrogle](https://www.astrogle.com/astrology/second-marriage-divorce-in-astrology.html); [jagannathhora](https://jagannathhora.com/second-marriage-predictions-kp/)
- astrogle's own study of 100 charts with two marriages reports agreement of 74% (Rule 1, 7th CSL dual), 79% (Rule 2, 2nd CSL) and 93% (Rule 3, 9th CSL). It also says no rule is 100% reliable. The study is undocumented. — [astrogle](https://www.astrogle.com/astrology/second-marriage-divorce-in-astrology.html)
- 11th CSL for divorce (Quora practitioner): "If the 11th cuspal sub-lord signifies 6 or 10, it indicates divorce … when 6 or 10 planets come to Dasha or Anter Dasha, then the divorce will be committed during these periods." — [Quora](https://www.quora.com/How-do-you-know-that-native-married-life-will-be-separated-or-divorced-by-KP-astrology)
- The publisher's contents list for KSK Reader IV includes the question "Is second marriage promised?". This confirms the book covers the topic. I found no excerpt of the text. — [VedicBooks](https://www.vedicbooks.net/marriage-married-life-children-p-16508.html)

### Inferences
- The tested hierarchy, best supported first: (a) the 7th CSL gives the promise, and the dual-sign condition gives plurality. (b) The 2nd CSL linked to 7 (with 11) confirms a further legal marriage, a refinement added by later practitioners. (c) The 9th CSL is weak, supporting evidence at most.
- Only the 7th-CSL rule carries KSK's name in the sources. The 2nd-, 9th- and 11th-CSL tests come from later practitioners.

### Gaps
- I found no verified excerpt from Reader IV. I found nothing from K. Subramaniam, R. Sundaram or the KP & Astrology annual articles. No source I could reach quoted them on remarriage.

## Q2. The dual (common) sign rule for more than one marriage: exact wording

### Takeaway
The wording attributed to KSK is that the 7th CSL being **Mercury**, or **occupying a dual sign**, or **in the star of a planet in a dual sign** gives more than one marriage. The 7th *cusp itself* falling in a dual sign is **not** the condition. AstroSage and others add a second, conjunctive condition: the 7th CSL must **also signify 2 or 11**, or the second marriage is ruled out.

### Cited Findings
- Attributed to KSK: "If the 7th sub-lord is either **Mercury** or any planet **occupying a dual sign** or **deposited in the constellation of a planet in the dual sign**, there will be more then one marriage." — [astrogle](https://www.astrogle.com/astrology/second-marriage-divorce-in-astrology.html); [myastroconsult mirror](http://myastroconsult.blogspot.com/2013/05/second-marriage-and-divorce-issues-in.html)
- AstroSage KP Rules (7th house) uses a **two-part conjunctive** rule: "the sub-lord of the 7th cusp is deposited in a dual sign or in the constellation of a planet in **or owning** a dual sign or if the sub-lord of the 7th cusp is Mercury; and … the sub-lord of the 7th cusp is also a significator of either of the houses 2 or 11." If either part fails, there is no second marriage. — [AstroSage KP 7th House](https://kpastrology.astrosage.com/kp-learning-home/kp-rules/7th-house)
- Another summary says the same: "Both the above principles must be satisfied together for second marriage to take place." Also: "The seventh cuspal sublord has to signify either 2nd or 11th houses." — [Scribd 2nd marriage divorce](https://www.scribd.com/document/592038252/2nd-marriage-divorce)
- astrogle's "Rule 1" version: "The sub-lord of the 7th cusp **OR its Starlord** is deposited in a dual sign", plus signification of 2 or 11. "For this study" it takes Gemini, Virgo and Pisces as dual signs (it **omits Sagittarius**) and Mercury and Jupiter as dual planets. — [astrogle](https://www.astrogle.com/astrology/second-marriage-divorce-in-astrology.html)
- A variant (Vedic Raj Astrology): "the 7th sub lord is either mercury or Jupiter or 7th sub lord is placed in sign of Jupiter or Mercury and signifies 5, 7 and 11 houses the native will marry more than one". — [vedicrajastrology](https://www.vedicrajastrology.com/2020/09/second-marriage-in-astrology/)
- A caution: Mercury's dual nature alone does not independently confirm a second marriage. — [jagannathhora](https://jagannathhora.com/second-marriage-predictions-kp/) (via search summary)

### Inferences
- Rule set to apply to computed data, written as data checks:
  - (i) The 7th CSL is Mercury, OR its sign is dual (Gemini, Virgo, Sagittarius, Pisces), OR its star-lord sits in a dual sign. AstroSage also accepts a star-lord that owns a dual sign, i.e. Mercury or Jupiter as nakshatra lord.
  - (ii) AND the 7th CSL signifies 2 or 11.
- Points of disagreement:
  - Is Sagittarius a dual sign? Standard astrology says yes. astrogle's study omits it.
  - Does "owning a dual sign" count? AstroSage says yes. KSK's attributed wording does not mention it.
  - Is condition (ii) required? KSK's attributed wording does not have it. AstroSage and the Scribd summary do.

### Gaps
- I could not confirm whether the "and also signifies 2 or 11" clause is KSK's own text or a later addition.

## Q3. Is house 2 fulfilment or negation for a second marriage? Which houses negate?

### Takeaway
In mainstream KP, **2 is a fulfilment house**: it brings a family addition and is part of the 2-7-11 set for marriage and for a second marriage. The negation houses are **1, 6 and 10**, as the 12th from 2, 7 and 11. Some practitioners also read the 2nd as **8th from the 7th** (the spouse's longevity). For them, a 7th-CSL link to 2 (or 6) shows the *end* of the first marriage, by the spouse's death (2) or by separation (6). That clears the way for a second marriage rather than denying it.

### Cited Findings
- "1 (12th from 2), 6 (12th from 7) and 10 (12th from 11) will give opposite results, i.e. denial of marriage or separation, divorce etc., if already married." — AstroSage KP tutorial, Chapter 2, quoted in a search snippet ([AstroSage Ch. 2](https://kpastrology.astrosage.com/kp-learning-home/tutorial/chapter-2-fundamental-principles))
- The 7th CSL signifying 2 and 11 promises marriage. Signifying 1, 6 or 10 usually denies it. — [astrogle, Marriage, married life, children and divorce](https://www.astrogle.com/astrology/marriage-married-life-children-and-divorce.html)
- The 2nd as the 8th from the 7th: "2nd house is 8th (i.e. house of age) from 7th (spouse) and indicates age of spouse and therefore malefics in this house indicate danger to spouse." — [astroshastra, Death of Spouse](https://www.astroshastra.com/articles/deathofspouse.php)
- "For second marriage to happen legally, first marriage has to break through divorce or death of 1st spouse, which can be known to happen if sublord of 7th house is connected to 2nd house or 6th house" (the 2nd as 8th from 7th, the 6th as 12th from 7th). — [Scribd 2nd marriage divorce](https://www.scribd.com/document/592038252/2nd-marriage-divorce); [heavenschild](https://www.heavenschild.com.au/k-astrology/kp-astrology-second-marriage.html)
- Marriage can be denied when the 7th CSL signifies only negative houses (1, 6, 10; one page also lists 4) and none of 2, 7 or 11. Any 2, 7 or 11 signification prevents outright denial, even when negative houses are also signified. — [astrogle, 8th house for marriage or divorce in KP](https://www.astrogle.com/astrology/8th-house-for-marriage-or-divorce-in-kp.html)
- Some practitioner sites use 6-8-12 rather than 1-6-10 as the obstruction set. — [jagannathhora](https://jagannathhora.com/timing-marriage-2-7-11-formula/); [panchangbodh](https://panchangbodh.com/kp-sub-lord-calculator)

### Inferences
- For the engine reading, treat 2 as fulfilment, in line with KSK's 2-7-11 doctrine. The 2nd-as-8th-from-7th reading is a later overlay used to explain how the *first* marriage ended. It is not a reason to deny the second.
- The 12th is not part of KSK's 1-6-10 negation set. The practitioner sources use it for separation or bed-pleasure loss (see Q4), not denial.

### Gaps
- I found no KSK-text quotation on whether 2 negates a *second* marriage specifically. None of the sources treated 2 as negation for the second marriage.

## Q4. A 7th CSL signifying 1, 2 and 7: marriage promised with separation? Can a later daśā give a further marriage?

### Takeaway
The practitioner consensus: when the 7th CSL signifies both fulfilment houses (2, 7, 11) and negation houses (1, 6, 10), **marriage takes place, and so does a later break** (separation or divorce). The dasha shows which comes when: periods of 2-7-11 significators give the marriage, periods of 1-6-10 significators give the break. Remarriage needs a further 2-7-11 daśā/bhukti after the break, plus the plurality conditions (Q2) or the 2nd-CSL link to 7 (Q1). Sources disagree on whether 6 or 10 in the 7th CSL means separation or divorce.

### Cited Findings
- Partha Pratim Mitra (practitioner): "if the seventh cuspal sublord … denotes either of 1, 6, or 10 then divorce or break in marriage is clearly promised"; 12 denotes separation; if the 7th CSL signifies both 6 and 7 or 11, both marriage and the later divorce are indicated; "If the seventh cuspal sublord denotes 8 and 12 alongwith 6 or 1 or 10 then litigation is going to happen after marriage." — [LinkedIn, Mitra](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/promise-marriage-second-alongwith-marital-issues-kp-mitra-fca)
- Contrary view (Quora): "If the 7th cuspal sub lord signifies 6 or 10, it means separation life but not divorce". Divorce is shown instead by the 11th CSL signifying 6 or 10. — [Quora](https://www.quora.com/How-do-you-know-that-native-married-life-will-be-separated-or-divorced-by-KP-astrology)
- A third view: divorce when the 7th CSL signifies 1 and 6, or 6 and 10, together with an afflicted Venus. — via search summary of [hkastrologygroup](https://hkastrologygroup.com/marriage-married-life-children-and-divorce-kp-system/) and [muhuratchoghadiya](https://www.muhuratchoghadiya.com/kundali/faq/kp_marriage_prediction?lang=en) (I could not tell which of the two says this)
- Timing: separation or divorce becomes likely when Dasha-Bhukti periods activate the separation houses while the marriage-sustaining houses are inactive. "If separation is indicated but second marriage is also supported, the person may divorce and remarry." — [jagannathhora, Divorce and Separation](https://jagannathhora.com/divorce-separation-indicators-kp/); [jagannathhora, Second Marriage](https://jagannathhora.com/second-marriage-predictions-kp/)
- The first marriage should be confirmed as ended before remarriage is analysed. — [jagannathhora](https://jagannathhora.com/second-marriage-predictions-kp/)
- Sources disagree on the star-lord's role. One says the cusp's star-lord plays no part in the verdict. Another includes the CSL's star-lord and sub-lord. — via search summary of [Quora](https://www.quora.com/How-do-you-know-that-native-married-life-will-be-separated-or-divorced-by-KP-astrology) / [muhuratchoghadiya](https://www.muhuratchoghadiya.com/kundali/faq/kp_marriage_prediction?lang=en)

### Inferences
- In the 1-2-7 case, house 1 is the 12th from 2 and negates only the family-addition part. 7 is intact, so the promise stands, with a built-in tendency to self-will or separation. Whether a *further* marriage follows depends on the plurality markers (Q2 and the 2nd CSL), not on the 1-2-7 promise alone. One promise does not automatically license several marriages.
- For a "WHEN" reading, see `docs/AI_EVENT_TIMING_GUIDE.md`. KP gives the yes/no and the quality. The timing comes from the daśā clock and transits.

### Gaps
- I found no KSK-original statement on the 1+2+7 combination or on whether a single promise covers a remarriage.

## Q5. Does denial by the 7th CSL mean unmarried for life, and does an existing promise make remarriage possible?

### Takeaway
I found **no verified KSK quotation** that a native denied by the 7th CSL "remains unmarried for life". Secondary sources state the logic in general terms: the 7th CSL signifying only 1, 6 and 10 (no 2, 7 or 11) means denial, and the person stays single. The converse, that an existing promise makes remarriage possible, is **not** stated as a blanket rule. Remarriage needs its own markers: the dual-sign 7th CSL plus 2 or 11, and/or the 2nd CSL signifying 7 (with 11).

### Cited Findings
- "If 2, 7, 11 sub-lords connect only to 1, 6, 10 significators, the person will remain single." — practitioner summary via search snippet ([jagannathhora, Denial of Marriage](https://jagannathhora.com/?p=346))
- The 6-10-12 pattern is described both as a near-denial and as "not a death sentence" (delay or complication rather than an absolute bar). The sources disagree on how strict it is. — [jagannathhora, Denial](https://jagannathhora.com/?p=346); [jagannathhora, Is marriage promised](https://jagannathhora.com/is-marriage-promised-in-my-chart/)
- Denial requires that **none** of 2, 7 or 11 is signified. Any of them prevents outright denial. — [astrogle, 8th house in KP](https://www.astrogle.com/astrology/8th-house-for-marriage-or-divorce-in-kp.html)
- If either condition of the conjunctive dual-sign rule fails, a second marriage is denied. — [AstroSage KP 7th House](https://kpastrology.astrosage.com/kp-learning-home/kp-rules/7th-house); [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/592038252/2nd-marriage-divorce)
- A promise must still be activated by the daśā running at the time. — [jagannathhora 2-7-11](https://jagannathhora.com/timing-marriage-2-7-11-formula/)

### Inferences
- For a divorced native, the first marriage already happened, so the 7th CSL promise is established empirically. The live question is the *plurality* markers and a later 2-7-11 daśā. Under the AstroSage conjunctive rule, failing the plurality test is read as no second marriage. Under the looser 2nd-CSL rule (Jagannath Hora), the 2nd CSL can still promise one. Present both views; do not merge them into one verdict.

### Gaps
- I could not find the "unmarried for life" wording in Reader IV, or any text from K. Subramaniam or R. Sundaram on this. The texts would need a physical or scanned check.
