# Parashari and Jaimini doctrine on the second spouse / second marriage

> Research method note: every fetch of a primary text page (enjoylearningsanskrit.com BPHS verses, bphs.blogspot.com Ch.18, wisdomlib Phaladeepika Ch.10, shrifreedom.org, srath.com) failed in this environment (DNS / proxy 403). All findings below come from search-engine extracts of those pages. Wording is therefore close paraphrase or short extract, not a verified verbatim quote, and verse numbers vary across editions. Anything I know from memory but could not source is placed under Inferences or Gaps and labelled as such. Sanskrit verse text could not be retrieved.

## Q1. Parashari texts (BPHS, Phaladeepika, Saravali, Jataka Parijata, Uttara Kalamrita): which house shows the second spouse, and which yogas give more than one marriage

### Takeaway
The best-supported classical answer for "which house is the second spouse" is the **8th house in the Parashari texts, read as the 2nd from the 7th** (Uttara Kalamrita; Rath restates it). The 9th (3rd from the 7th) and the 11th are modern readings or additions, and the classical sources I could check don't back them as the "second-spouse house". The multiple-marriage yogas are well attested: Venus and/or the 7th lord in a dual sign or dual navāṁśa (Phaladeepika, BPHS 18), a weak, debilitated or malefic-afflicted 7th lord (BPHS 18), Venus+Moon or Venus+Mercury in the 7th (Saravali), and a eunuch planet in the 7th with two planets in the 11th (Phaladeepika).

### Cited Findings
**Which house shows the second wife (classical)**
- Uttara Kalamrita (Kalidasa; P.S. Sastri translation) says that **information about the second wife should be sought through the 8th house**. The edition has a section headed "Information about second wife". — [Uttara Kalamrita, Sastri tr. (archive.org PDF)](https://dn721906.ca.archive.org/0/items/kupdf.net_uttkalamrita-kalidas-ps-sastri/kupdf.net_uttkalamrita-kalidas-ps-sastri.pdf); [Uttarakalamritam English tr. PDF](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/UTTARAKALAMRITAM.pdf)
- Sanjay Rath (modern, SJC) puts it this way: "the second marriage is seen from the **eighth from Upapada and eighth from seventh house (i.e. second house)**". In the bhāva chart the Parashari counterpart of the 8th-from-UL rule is therefore the 8th from the 7th, which is the natal **2nd house**. — [Sanjay Rath on Marriage (Scribd excerpt)](https://www.scribd.com/document/265405739/Marriage-Sanjay-Rath); [Rath, Darapada and Upapada (srath.com)](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/darapada-and-upapada/)
- **Disagreement between the two rules.** Uttara Kalamrita names the natal **8th house** for the second wife. Rath's Parashari counterpart is "8th from 7th = **2nd house**". These are two different houses, so the derivation has to be stated explicitly when the data is read. (Both sources above.)
- Modern blogs (not classical) offer the **9th house** as "3rd from the 7th", meaning the next partner after the 7th. One of them admits this is "not a universal rule". — [astrologertripathi.com, 9th house second chances](https://astrologertripathi.com/2026/09/07/second-marriage-astrology-9th-house-second-chances/); [astromedha.in](https://astromedha.in/insights/vedic/second-marriage-in-astrology)
- Modern blogs give the **11th house** two roles: where the second spouse is met (social circle), and "two or more planets [in the 11th] can signify more than one marriage". — [astrotalk blog](https://astrotalk.com/astrology-blog/how-to-know-if-you-have-a-second-marriage-in-your-horoscope/); [astromitra](https://www.astromitra.com/second-marriage-astrology-chart.php)
- Modern sources also disagree about the 8th (as 2nd from 7th, the spouse's wealth) versus the 2nd (family after the first marriage), and several recommend synthesising the 7th, 2nd, 8th, 9th, Venus, Jupiter, D9, daśā and transits rather than relying on one house. — [astromedha.in](https://astromedha.in/insights/vedic/second-marriage-in-astrology); [grahaguru.in](https://grahaguru.in/questions/second-marriage-kundli)

**BPHS Ch.18 (Yuvati/Kalatra Bhāva) multiple-wife and loss-of-wife combinations (Santhanam-based English)**
- **Two wives:** the 7th lord is "in fall, or in a malefic rāśi along with a malefic, while Yuvati Bhāva, or the 7th navāṁśa belong to a eunuch planet" (eunuch planets are Mercury and Saturn). — [BPHS English PDF (vedpuran.net)](https://vedpuran.net/wp-content/uploads/2021/04/brihat_parashara_hora_shastra_english_v.pdf); [BPHS PDF (lakshminarayanlenasia)](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/BPHS.pdf)
- **Three wives:** Mars and Venus in the 7th, or Saturn in the 7th, while the Lagna lord is in the 8th (Randhra). — same BPHS PDFs as above.
- **Many spouses (BPHS 18.21, per a word-by-word gloss):** a **strong 7th lord with Venus in a dual (dvisvabhāva) sign** gives one "endowed with many spouses". The gloss places the dual-sign condition on **Venus**. Later writers extend it to the 7th lord or the 7th house. — [enjoylearningsanskrit BPHS 18.21](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-18/verse-21) (search extract); extensions in [astromitra](https://www.astromitra.com/second-marriage-astrology-chart.php) and [indastro](https://www.indastro.com/astrology-articles/second-marriage-in-astrology.html)
- **Weak or afflicted 7th lord gives a sickly wife and many wives:** "In case the 7th lord is weak, is in his sign of debilitation, is in an enemy's sign or is combust, the wife … is sickly and he will have many wives." A separate verse says: 7th lord in a sign of Saturn or Venus, aspected by a benefic, gives many wives. — [aaps.space, 7th house/7th lord wife indications](https://aaps.space/docs/spouse/7h-7l-wife-int/); [jyotishvidya.com Ch.18](http://jyotishvidya.com/ch18.htm)
- **Loss (death) of wife:** the 7th lord is devoid of strength and in the 6th, 8th or 12th, or in fall, so "the wife will be destroyed (die early)". Venus conjunct a malefic in any house causes loss of wife. — [jyotishvidya.com Ch.18](http://jyotishvidya.com/ch18.htm); [bphs.blogspot Ch.18](http://bphs.blogspot.com/2007/12/ch-18-effects-of-7th-house.html) (search extract)
- **Lagna lord in the 7th:** a BPHS sūtra uses the word **"dvibhāryaḥ"** (two wives). Revati Astro reads it as probabilistic, not literal. I could not verify the Sanskrit independently. — [Revati Astro, 1st lord in 7th](https://revatiastro.com/1st-lord-in-7th-house/)
- **Venus in the 11th / malefics in the 2nd and 7th with the 7th lord / 7th lord in the 6th or 12th** gives more than one wife. This is attributed to **Bhāvārtha Ratnākara** and a learning series, **not** to BPHS. — [Medium, Bhava Phala Praveshika 9](https://medium.com/thoughts-on-jyotish/bhava-phala-praveshika-9-dara-bhava-7th-house-80ccef39f9b6); [vedastro, Bhavartha Ratnakara Part 20](https://vedastro.org/blog/Bhavartha-Ratnakara-Part-20-Seventh-House-Indications.html)
- The search found **no BPHS Ch.18 verse that makes the 11th lord the indicator of a second wife**. — [search result summary over Santhanam-based PDFs](https://lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/BPHS.pdf)

**Phaladeepika Ch.10 (Kalatra Bhāva), Mantreśvara**
- "If the lord of the 7th house and Venus be each posited in a **dual rāśi or aṁśa**, the person will have **two wives**." The number of wives is "estimated from the number of planets in conjunction with" the 7th lord and Venus. — [wisdomlib, Phaladeepika Ch.10](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/phaladeepika-by-mantreswara-text-and-translation/d/doc1621582.html); [pallassanas blog Ch.10](http://pallassanas.blogspot.com/2012/09/phaladeepika-of-manthreswara-chapter-10_6.html)
- A **eunuch (hermaphrodite) planet in the 7th plus two planets in the 11th** gives two wives. This is the classical root of the modern "11th house shows second marriage" claim. — [wisdomlib, Phaladeepika Ch.10](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/phaladeepika-by-mantreswara-text-and-translation/d/doc1621582.html)
- Each of the following is read as loss of wife (some translations say "crippled wife" instead):
  - Venus with a malefic in the 5th, 7th or 9th;
  - malefics in the 12th, 4th or 8th from Venus;
  - Venus hemmed between malefics;
  - the 7th or the 7th lord associated with, aspected by or hemmed between malefics.
  — [wisdomlib, Phaladeepika Ch.10](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/phaladeepika-by-mantreswara-text-and-translation/d/doc1621582.html); [astropankajseth, Phaldipika Ch.10](https://astropankajseth.co.in/2018/02/phaldipika-chapter-10/)

**Saravali (Kalyāṇa Varmā), 7th-house chapter (about Ch.34, vv.45–48; numbering varies)**
- v.48: if the 7th has many divisions (vargas) of **Venus and the Moon**, or is occupied by them, there is a plurality of wives. **Mercury and Venus together in the 7th** also give a plurality of wives. — [jyotisa.wordpress, corpus of Saravali](https://jyotisa.wordpress.com/2013/11/30/the-corpus-of-kalyana-varma-a-k-a-saravali/); [Saravali PDF](https://www.lakshminarayanlenasia.com/articles/Saravali.pdf)
- v.47: the number of wives corresponds to the number of planets **aspecting the 7th** (or the navāṁśa division in the 7th). There is a **single wife** if Leo, Aries or Scorpio rises in the navāṁśa (of the 7th), or with Mercury and Jupiter together in the 7th. — [jyotisa.wordpress](https://jyotisa.wordpress.com/2013/11/30/the-corpus-of-kalyana-varma-a-k-a-saravali/)

**Jataka Parijata**
- Ch.10 of Jataka Parijata is the **Aṣṭakavarga** chapter (72 verses). The 7th/8th/9th-house material is in **Ch.XIV**. A blog's claim that a "Dvibhāryā" yoga sits in "section 10" doesn't match this chapter list. — [Wikipedia, Jataka Parijata](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jataka_Parijata); [astropankajseth, house meanings per Jatak Parijat](https://astropankajseth.co.in/2016/10/house-meaning-according-to-jatak-parijat/)

### Inferences
- Taken together, the classical Parashari "more than one marriage" signals are: (a) **dual-sign or dual-navāṁśa** placement of Venus and/or the 7th lord; (b) a **debilitated, combust, weak, dusthāna-placed or malefic-hemmed 7th lord**; (c) **Venus afflicted** by malefics; (d) the 7th or its navāṁśa belonging to a **eunuch planet (Mercury/Saturn)**; (e) **count of planets** with the 7th lord/Venus, or aspecting the 7th (Saravali, Phaladeepika); (f) Phaladeepika's eunuch planet in the 7th plus two planets in the 11th. All of these are chart-readable from already-computed D1/D9 data and need no new engine number.
- Many of these combinations stand for **loss** (death or separation) of the first spouse, from which a second marriage follows, rather than for "two spouses" directly. When reading, keep "first marriage broken" separate from "second marriage promised".
- The 9th-house (3rd from 7th) reading follows the bhāvāt bhāvam logic, but I found no classical verse naming the 9th as the second wife. Treat it as a modern derivation.
- From memory, unverified: many traditional teachers also cite **Saturn's or Rahu's influence on the 7th/7th lord** and **Mars-Venus links** as remarriage indicators. I could not source these to a specific verse here.

### Gaps
- No verbatim Sanskrit or exact Santhanam verse numbers could be retrieved for BPHS Ch.18 (the primary-text pages were unreachable). Verse numbers above are approximate.
- Jataka Parijata Ch.XIV verses on two wives were not retrieved.
- Saravali verse numbering differs by edition (Santhanam vs. others), so it is unconfirmed.

## Q2. Jaimini / Upapada (UL): which sign from UL shows the second marriage; malefics in the 2nd from UL; Jupiter's protection; debilitated or exalted planets

### Takeaway
Jaimini and BPHS Ch.30 make the **2nd from UL** the sustainer or breaker of the marriage. Malefics there (classically Saturn/Rahu) without benefic aspect break the marriage, and benefic conjunction or aspect prevents the break. Sanjay Rath's school reads the **next spouse from the 8th from UL** (the "second UL"), then the 8th from that for a third, and so on. A **debilitated** planet in the 2nd from UL harms or causes loss of the spouse. An **exalted** planet there gives "many wives". **Gemini** (a dual sign) is also cited classically for many spouses, but this is from memory and unverified here.

### Cited Findings
- Rath defines the Upapada as the ārūḍha of the 12th house, the "Vivāha Maṇḍapa" (marriage altar). — [Rath, Darapada and Upapada (srath.com)](https://srath.com/jyoti%E1%B9%A3a/scholar/darapada-and-upapada/)
- **Second marriage = 8th from UL** (also 8th from the 7th bhāva = 2nd house): "the second marriage is seen from the eighth from Upapada and eighth from seventh house (i.e. second house)". — [Sanjay Rath on Marriage (Scribd)](https://www.scribd.com/document/265405739/Marriage-Sanjay-Rath)
- A forum summary of Rath's position: "the Upapada doesn't change but the house showing the second (or subsequent) marriage changes". This is not authoritative. — [LightOnVedicAstrology forum, "UL change"](https://phpbb.lightonvedicastrology.com/viewtopic.php?t=38804)
- The rule chains: count **8 signs from UL to get UL2**, and 8 from UL2 to get UL3, and so on. Several sources add that the 8th from UL describes the **character of a later relationship if one occurs**. It doesn't by itself predict that the first marriage ends. — [jagannathhora, UL spouse guide](https://jagannathhora.com/upapada-lagna-spouse-appearance/); [goodstarsjyotish substack](https://goodstarsjyotish.substack.com/p/the-upapada-the-marriage-point-in)
- **Terminology conflict:** in Rath's school "UL2" means the 8th from UL, and the 2nd from UL is read for the marriage's sustenance. Other writers use "A2/UL2" for a separately derived point. — [jagannathhora](https://jagannathhora.com/upapada-lagna-spouse-appearance/)
- **2nd from UL as the timing of the break:** Rath says the 2nd from UL "can determine the timing of the actual breakage in marriage", meaning when separation becomes publicly acknowledged through divorce or death. — [Sanjay Rath on Marriage (Scribd)](https://www.scribd.com/document/265405739/Marriage-Sanjay-Rath)
- A practitioner (not a sūtra) says divorces typically occur when **Chara daśā activates the sign 2nd from UL**. — [astrosight, UL marriage timing](https://astrosight.ai/kundli/upapada-lagna-marriage-timing); [blog.indianastrologysoftware](https://blog.indianastrologysoftware.com/upapada-and-marriage/)
- **Saturn + Rahu in the 2nd from UL:** the spouse is lost or deserted through scandal, calamity or death. Shri Freedom cites this as JS 1.4.6. Benefic aspects soften it (cited as 1.4.22). A **benefic conjunction or aspect on the 2nd from UL may prevent loss of spouse or renunciation** (cited as 1.4.2). — [shrifreedom.org, Upapada Lagna](https://shrifreedom.org/vedic-astrology/upapada-lagna/)
- **Sūtra-number conflict:** other sources cite **1.4.6** as "an exalted planet in the 2nd from UL (or the 2nd in an exalted navāṁśa) gives **many wives**". One adds "or she is long-lived". — [Science of Light, Parashara and Jaimini (uses Rath's translation)](https://www.scienceoflight.net/science-of-light-course/classical-vedic-astrology/parashara-and-jaimini/); [blog.indianastrologysoftware](https://blog.indianastrologysoftware.com/upapada-and-marriage/)
- **Debilitated planet in the 2nd from UL** harms the wife (loss). — [Science of Light](https://www.scienceoflight.net/science-of-light-course/classical-vedic-astrology/parashara-and-jaimini/)
- JS 1.4.8: if the 2nd lord (from UL) is in its own house or exalted, or conjoined by its own lord, loss of spouse comes only in old age, "if at all". — [Science of Light](https://www.scienceoflight.net/science-of-light-course/classical-vedic-astrology/parashara-and-jaimini/)
- JS 1.4.9–10: an exalted UL lord gives a spouse from an upper-class family, and a debilitated one gives a lower-class family. Parāśara (BPHS 30.13–15) adds that an exalted 7th lord or Venus gives the same. — [Science of Light](https://www.scienceoflight.net/science-of-light-course/classical-vedic-astrology/parashara-and-jaimini/)
- BPHS Ch.30 (Ārūḍha chapter) restates the Upapada doctrine, including the importance of the 2nd from UL for supporting marriage (verses about 30.8–30.15). Difficult placements of the **UL lord in the 6th, 8th or 12th from UL** are read as strain. — [goodstarsjyotish substack](https://goodstarsjyotish.substack.com/p/the-upapada-the-marriage-point-in); [enjoylearningsanskrit BPHS 30.15](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-30/verse-15) (search extract only)
- **Jupiter as protector** (modern): one benefic, especially Jupiter, aspecting a malefic configuration on the 2nd from UL "can save the marriage at the last minute". Two or more malefics with no benefic aspect make divorce likely. "Jupiter in 2nd from UL prevents divorce." Benefics (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, Moon) in the 2nd from UL give a long-lasting marriage. — [star-meet.com](https://star-meet.com/blog/upapada-lagna-vedic-astrology-spouse); [astroword.in](https://www.astroword.in/upapada-lagna); [jagannathhora](https://jagannathhora.com/upapada-lagna-spouse-appearance/). Star-Meet attributes the Saturn-Rahu rule to BPHS/K.N. Rao rather than Jaimini, which is another attribution conflict.
- Rath's edition: *Jaimini Maharishi's Upadeśa Sūtras*, Sanskrit text, English translation and comments, Sagar Publications, 2002. — [exoticindiaart listing](https://www.exoticindiaart.com/book/details/jaimini-maharishi-s-upadesa-sutras-idj587/); [srath.com](https://srath.com/misc/jaimini-maharishis-upadesa-sutra/)

### Inferences
- From memory of Rath's Upadeśa numbering, not verified in this session:
  - 1.4.2: a malefic in or aspecting the 2nd from UL gives pravrajyā or dāranāśa (renunciation or loss of wife).
  - 1.4.3: a benefic conjunction or aspect means not.
  - 1.4.4: the Sun is not counted as a malefic there if exalted or in a friend's sign, or with a benefic aspect.
  - 1.4.5: a debilitated planet gives loss of wife.
  - 1.4.6: an exalted planet gives many wives.
  - 1.4.7: Gemini in the 2nd from UL gives many wives.

  This would put 1.4.6 at "exalted gives many wives", which matches the Science of Light citation. If so, the Shri Freedom "1.4.6 = Saturn/Rahu" label is probably a numbering slip. A downstream writer should cite the rule content, not the sūtra number.
- Classical texts make a paradox explicit: **exaltation** in the 2nd from UL is said to give *more than one* spouse, not just a strong marriage. So "strong 2nd from UL" does not mean "single lasting marriage". Read it together with the dual-sign signal.
- Applied to a second marriage: the Jaimini test for whether the second marriage lasts is the same 2nd-from-UL test, applied to the **2nd from the 8th-from-UL** (that is, the 9th from the natal UL). This follows from the Rath chaining logic. It is an inference, not a quoted rule.
- The "12th from UL" as a separation indicator didn't appear in any source found. Don't use it as doctrine.

### Gaps
- No verbatim Jaimini Sanskrit was retrieved, and sūtra numbers conflict across sources (see above).
- I found nothing attributable to K.N. Rao's own text on second-marriage UL rules. The only Rao-attributed claim found concerned synastry (partner's luminaries aspecting your UL), not second marriage.
- The proper Jaimini divorce-timing method (Chara daśā on the 2nd from UL) came only from practitioner blogs. Rath's own timing text was not retrieved.

## Q3. How the schools say a broken first marriage is followed by a lasting second (durability signs for the second-spouse house)

### Takeaway
No retrieved classical verse gives an explicit "first breaks, second endures" rule. Durability is inferred by applying each school's normal marriage-sustenance test to whichever house or sign stands for the second spouse. In Jaimini that is benefic or Jupiter influence and the absence of Saturn/Rahu/debilitated planets on the 2nd from that UL. In Parashari it is the strength and benefic association of the second-spouse house (the 8th per Uttara Kalamrita, or the 2nd per Rath's 8th-from-7th) and its lord, together with the D9 and Venus.

### Cited Findings
- Benefic conjunction or aspect on the 2nd from UL prevents loss of spouse or renunciation. Benefics there give a long-lasting marriage. Jupiter in the 2nd from UL prevents divorce. — [shrifreedom.org](https://shrifreedom.org/vedic-astrology/upapada-lagna/); [star-meet.com](https://star-meet.com/blog/upapada-lagna-vedic-astrology-spouse); [astroword.in](https://www.astroword.in/upapada-lagna)
- The 2nd from UL lord in its own sign or exalted means loss only in old age, if at all (JS 1.4.8, Rath translation per Science of Light). — [Science of Light](https://www.scienceoflight.net/science-of-light-course/classical-vedic-astrology/parashara-and-jaimini/)
- The 8th from UL describes the character of a later relationship *if one occurs*. Second-marriage analysis "requires reading the full chart rather than the UL in isolation". — [jagannathhora](https://jagannathhora.com/upapada-lagna-spouse-appearance/)
- Saravali's "single wife" conditions apply to the 7th: Leo, Aries or Scorpio navāṁśa in the 7th, or Mercury and Jupiter together in the 7th. — [jyotisa.wordpress](https://jyotisa.wordpress.com/2013/11/30/the-corpus-of-kalyana-varma-a-k-a-saravali/)
- A modern article frames the second marriage as repeating natal patterns (the Dārakāraka's "curriculum") rather than a clean slate. — [jagannathhora, "Will a second marriage be better?"](https://jagannathhora.com/tag/marriage-astrology/)

### Inferences
- A domain-general reading recipe that is consistent with the sources (interpretive, not engine rules):
  1. **Break of the first marriage.** Look for affliction of the 2nd from UL (Saturn/Rahu or debilitated planets, no benefic aspect), weakness or affliction of the 7th lord or Venus (BPHS 18; Phaladeepika 10), and dual-sign or dual-navāṁśa placement of Venus or the 7th lord.
  2. **Promise of the second.** Look at the 8th from UL and the Parashari second-spouse house. Uttara Kalamrita names the 8th; Rath's 8th-from-7th rule gives the 2nd. Flag both, because the schools disagree. Also check the planet count with or aspecting the 7th lord or Venus (Saravali, Phaladeepika).
  3. **Durability of the second.** Benefic (especially Jupiter) association with the sign 2nd from the second-marriage UL, its lord dignified, and no Saturn/Rahu or debilitated planet there. In Parashari, the second-spouse house and its lord strong and benefic-aspected.
- A later (second) marriage that lasts should, on doctrine, show protective benefic influence on the second-marriage sustenance point that is *absent* from the first UL's 2nd house. That contrast is the triangulation signal, not a score.

### Gaps
- No classical verse (BPHS, Phaladeepika, Saravali, Jaimini) was found that explicitly addresses durability of the *second* marriage. Everything in this section is extrapolation of first-marriage rules.
- K.N. Rao's case-study writing on remarriage could not be found or retrieved.
