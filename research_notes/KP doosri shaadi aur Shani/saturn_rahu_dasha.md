## Q1. Saturn's role in relationships — stabiliser/"fixer" vs delayer/separator; does it "delay-but-sustain"?

### Takeaway
The "Saturn delays but does not deny, and what it gives it makes durable" view is a widespread **modern** reading (attributed to B.V. Raman by secondary blogs), while the **classical** layer found here only establishes Saturn as āyuṣkāraka (longevity significator) and a mostly malefic/delaying planet; no classical verse found saying Saturn "sustains" marriage. The durable outcome is conditional on Saturn's dignity and affliction.

### Cited Findings
**Classical layer (as relayed by secondary sources)**
- Saturn is the kāraka of longevity; an Uttara Kālāmṛta summary: "Saturn being the karaka for longevity, should not be adversely placed and become the cause of weakness of the 3 bhavas [1, 8, 10] or their lords. If that happens, Saturn becomes the primary Killer (Maraka)." — [LinkedIn summary of Uttara Kalamrita / longevity sources](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/35-significations-lagna-bhava-from-uttara-kalamrita-varaha-mihira); [Light in Life — Combinations for longevity](https://lightinlife.home.blog/2021/04/19/combinations-for-longevity-part-1/)
- Kālidāsa's Uttara Kālāmṛta assigns roughly 95 significations to Saturn, "most of these qualities are bad or on the negative side" (blog characterisation). — [Indian Astrology Software blog, "Much Misunderstood Saturn"](https://blog.indianastrologysoftware.com/much-misunderstood-saturn/)
- Phaladīpikā (as quoted by a blog): "Saturn in the Ascendant in exaltation or in own sign, the native will be like a king or head of a village or a town. If Saturn is in any other house, the native will be unhappy in his childhood and later will be poor and lazy." (Illustrates the classical dignity-dependence of Saturn's results; not a marriage verse.) — [Indian Astrology Software blog](https://blog.indianastrologysoftware.com/much-misunderstood-saturn/)
- A modern blog claims BPHS describes Saturn's 7th-house role as making marriage "labour-intensive but durable" (kāryakaraṇa / "work-doer") — this attribution is unverified and looks like the blog's paraphrase, not a BPHS verse. — [Astromata, "Saturn in 7th House Marriage: Delay or Strength? BPHS View"](https://astromata.com/blog/saturn-7th-house-marriage-delay-bphs/)
- No source surfaced that uses "sthira" as a classical term for Saturn's effect on marriage. — (negative finding; see [search summary sources above](https://jagannathhora.com/planets/saturn/))

**Modern opinion**
- B.V. Raman is quoted (by secondary blogs only) as saying "Saturn can delay but it is good for stability of Marriage" / "Saturn may delay marriage but gives it stability." — [Astroshastra, Late Marriage](https://www.astroshastra.com/Marriage%20Astrology/latemarriage.php); [Astrosanhita, Late Marriage](https://astrosanhita.com/late-marriage-or-delay-in-marriage-in-horoscope-astrology/) (attribution not verified against *How to Judge a Horoscope*)
- "Saturn in the 7th delays marriage but rarely denies it." — [Zodiacreads](https://zodiacreads.com/astrology/saturn-in-the-7th-house/)
- Delayed vs denied: "a delayed marriage and a denied marriage are two very different things in Vedic astrology"; when Saturn is strong "the marriage comes at the right maturity, the partner is dependable and loyal, and the union is stable for the long run"; as 7th lord Saturn "often gives a stable, lasting marriage once it does arrive." — [Astromedha, Why is my marriage getting delayed](https://astromedha.in/insights/relationships/why-is-my-marriage-getting-delayed); [Astromedha, Saturn mahadasha marriage](https://astromedha.in/insights/vedic/saturn-mahadasha-marriage)

### Inferences

- Saturn as a *pratyantar* lord at an engagement is consistent with the "Saturn fixes/formalises a commitment" reading only if Saturn's own condition (dignity, D9, link to 7th/2nd/11th) supports it; its longevity-kāraka role is a classical anchor for "endurance" but not specifically for marriage.

## Q2. KP — reading a dasha/pratyantar lord that signifies both a fulfilment house (11) and negation houses (8, 10, 12); sub-lord rule; "fixed-then-cancelled"

### Takeaway
In KP the star lord shows which houses a period lord works through, and the **sub lord decides** whether those matters come out favourable, unfavourable, delayed or denied; when a significator carries both 2-7-11 and negating houses (1-6-10, and in many writers 12, 6-8-12 for post-marriage separation), practitioners read it as "event happens but with delay/obstruction" or, if the sub lord leans to negation, as denial/breakage. House lists for negation are practitioner convention and vary.

### Cited Findings
- Sub lord as decider: the star lord shows which house matters are activated, but whether they come out favourable or unfavourable depends on the sub-lord; "When the star lord and sub lord disagree, the sub lord prevails"; it decides whether the result is "favourable, unfavourable, delayed or denied." — [RedAstrologer, Sub Lord in KP](https://redastrologer.com/what-is-sub-lord-in-kp-astrology-sub-lord-table/); [Rahasya Vedic Astrology, KP Demystified](https://www.rahasyavedicastrology.com/kp-astrology-demystified/); [Paramarsh, Star lords and significators](https://paramarsh.app/patrika/kp-astrology/kp-star-lord-significators)
- A planet "might have a star lord signifying favorable houses and a sub-lord signifying obstructing houses, or vice versa", in which case the sub lord generally decides; a partly unfavourable sub lord may leave the outcome mixed. — [Jagannath Hora, KP significator hierarchy](https://jagannathhora.com/kp-significator-hierarchy-4-level-reading/); [Paramarsh](https://paramarsh.app/patrika/kp-astrology/kp-star-lord-significators)
- KP basics: a planet gives results of its star lord's placement and owned houses. — [kpastrology.com basics](https://www.kpastrology.com/kpbasics.htm)
- Krishnamurti's teaching as relayed by a blog: "the planet whose sub lord and dasa are in operation is final." — [Astrology World blog (astrologeranjaan)](http://astrologeranjaan.blogspot.com/p/k-p-astrology.html)
- Marriage promise: 7th cuspal sub lord must signify 2, 7, 11 (Venus unafflicted); "if the sub-lord signifies 1 6 10 or does not signify 2 7 or 11, then the marriage is denied"; "if the sub-lord signifies both or a combination of both the houses then marriage will happen but with delay." — [Scribd, Marriage When according to KP](https://www.scribd.com/doc/150738899/Marriage-When-according-to-KP-Astrology); [Muhurat Choghadiya, KP Marriage Prediction](https://www.muhuratchoghadiya.com/kundali/faq/kp_marriage_prediction?lang=en); [Paramarsh, KP marriage timing](https://paramarsh.app/patrika/kp-astrology/kp-marriage-timing)
- Broken engagement: "when the 7th sub-lord signifies houses 6 (separation) or 12 (loss/foreign), KP warns of broken engagements, prolonged single status, or marital discord"; "if [it] signifies 6 and 12, partnership will break"; a case study attributes a broken engagement to a running period whose lord did not support marriage. — [Jagannath Hora, Marriage prediction in KP](https://jagannathhora.com/marriage-prediction-in-kp-astrology-complete-5-step-method-for-accurate-timing-using-jhora-with-real-examples/); [Astrology with Subir, "Why my engagement got broken twice"](https://astrologywithsubir.medium.com/why-my-engagement-got-broken-twice-as-per-astrology-a-case-study-2e2b7f04678d)
- Timing: joint dasha–bhukti–antara of the strongest significators of 2, 7, 11; "if the event is not promised then there is no point going to the Vimshottari dasa"; a bhukti lord strongly signifying the 6th was read as blocking marriage. — [Scribd KP marriage](https://www.scribd.com/doc/150738899/Marriage-When-according-to-KP-Astrology); [LightOnVedicAstrology forum](https://phpbb.lightonvedicastrology.com/viewtopic.php?t=38638)
- Disagreement: some writers list negation as 1-6-10 only, others add 12 or 6-8-12; others treat 5-8-12 (or 6-8-12) as separation **after** marriage rather than denial before it. — [same KP marriage sources, compared](https://paramarsh.app/patrika/kp-astrology/kp-marriage-timing)

### Inferences
- Applying to the given data (interpretive, not computed): Saturn PD signifying 11 (fulfilment of desire) alongside 10 (12th from 11 / 4th from 7 — part of 1-6-10 negation set), 12 and 8 is the textbook "mixed" significator — the shape practitioners describe as "event is triggered (11) but with obstruction/instability (10, 12, 8)". A "fixed-then-cancelled" or "fixed-then-delayed" reading is consistent with this shape, but KP says the deciding factor is the **sub lord** of Saturn (and of Rahu), plus whether the 7th CSL promises marriage at all — those must be read from the engine output, not assumed.
- Rahu AD signifying 12, 6, 10 with no 2/7/11 is, by these rules, a negating bhukti lord for marriage (6 = 12th from 7; 10 in the 1-6-10 set), unless its agent planets (Q3) bring in 2/7/11.
- Venus MD signifying 1, 2, 7 carries both a marriage house set (2, 7) and house 1 (12th from 2, part of the 1-6-10 negation set); the MD lord's own sub lord decides.
- The 5-8-12 cluster in Saturn (5 = romance; 8 and 12 often read as separation-after-marriage) fits "a relationship/engagement that is formed but faces breakage later" readings; this is inference.

### Gaps

- No authoritative KP source specifically addressing a pratyantar lord that is simultaneously 11 and 10/12 ("fixed then cancelled") was found.

## Q3. Rahu antardasha in Venus mahadasha — BPHS/Phaladīpikā wording; KP Rahu as agent

### Takeaway
BPHS (antardaśā-phala chapter of Venus, numbered ch. 60 in some editions) makes Rahu-in-Venus conditional: Rahu in kendra/trikoṇa/11th, exalted or benefic-aspected → great enjoyment, wealth, friends, successful journeys, land/cattle; otherwise inauspicious effects on self and parents, antagonism with people, physical distress. In KP, Rahu acts as an agent of conjoined planets, aspecting planets, its star lord and its sign lord (ordering disputed), and it "replaces" those planets.

### Cited Findings
**Classical (BPHS, as relayed by search snippets)**
- Opening condition of the Venus–Rahu verse: Rahu "situated in an angular (kendra), eleventh (lābha), or trinal (trikoṇa) house", exalted, aspected by benefics, or joined to a yoga-maker → favourable results. — [Enjoy Learning Sanskrit, Parashara ch. 60 v. 36](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-60/verse-36)
- Favourable results listed: "great enjoyment, gain of wealth, visits of friends, successful journeys, and gain of cattle and land"; adverse conditions: "inauspicious effects on oneself and one's parents, antagonism with people, and physical distress." — [BP Lama Jyotishavidya, BPHS Shukra Mahadasha](https://barbarapijan.com/bpa/VimshottariDasha/Zukra_Mahadasha_BPHS.htm) (snippet summary; chapter numbering varies between ch. 55 and ch. 60 across sites)
- Method stated by Santhanam's BPHS: antardaśā effects depend on the "dignities, indignities, strength and disposition of the Antardasa lords with reference to the Ascendant and the lord of the Dasa." — [Santhanam BPHS Vol. 2, archive.org](https://dn760006.eu.archive.org/0/items/BPHSEnglish/BPHS%20-%202%20RSanthanam.pdf)
- Duration: 20 × 18 / 120 = 3 years. — [Kalmanas, Venus–Rahu](https://www.kalmanas.com/dasha/venus-rahu-antardasha)
- Caution: BPHS's natural-friendship table covers the seven bodied grahas and omits the nodes, so Rahu's "friendship with Venus" comes from later literature. — [Jagannath Hora, Rahu MD Venus AD](https://jagannathhora.com/rahu-mahadasha-venus-antardasha-effects/)
- Analogy (different combination, Rahu AD in Saturn MD, BPHS ch. 57): beginning → enjoyment, wealth, family well-being; middle → cordiality with king; effects include "quarrels, mental agony, physical distress, antagonism with the sons, and discord with close relations"; remedies Mṛtyuñjaya japa, gift of a goat. Shows BPHS's pattern of phased (beginning/middle/end) Rahu sub-period results. — [BP Lama, BPHS Shani Mahadasha](https://www.barbarapijan.com/bpa/VimshottariDasha/Shani_Mahadasha_BPHS.htm)

**KP (practitioner restatements of Krishnamurti)**
- KP Reader II (as cited): "Rahu or Ketu will act as a strong agent to the planets to which they are conjoined. If they are not conjoined with any planet, then they give the results of the planet which aspects them." — [IndiaDivine thread, Rahu/Ketu as significators](https://www.indiadivine.org/content/topic/1435089-rahuketu-as-significators-different-views/)
- Order disputed: KP Reader VI order (conjunction, star lord, aspect, dispositor) vs KP Reader III (conjunction, aspect, star lord, dispositor), per the same thread; a Scribd outline gives conjoined → aspecting → own star lord → sign lord. — [IndiaDivine](https://www.indiadivine.org/content/topic/1435089-rahuketu-as-significators-different-views/); [Scribd, How to read Rahu Ketu in KP](https://www.scribd.com/document/117093248/How-to-Read-Rahu-Ketu-in-KP-Astrology)
- Nodes signify: houses occupied, houses of star lord (owned/occupied), houses of conjoined planets, houses of sign lord; "the star lord connection is the strongest." — [KP Astro App, house significators](https://kpastroapp.com/learn/house-significators-in-kp-astrology)
- Without conjunction/aspect, nodes "represent the sign lord where they are placed." — [Astrology HSK blog](http://astrologyhsk.blogspot.com/2014/11/rahu-ketu-and-their-significance-kp.html)
- Nodes "completely replace the Planets conjoined with it or aspecting it"; "nodes are ever stronger than the planets conjoined with them or aspected or the star lord or the depositor." — [kpastrology.com basics](https://www.kpastrology.com/kpbasics.htm); [RedAstrologer, Rahu and Ketu in KP](https://redastrologer.com/rahu-and-ketu-in-kp-astrology/)
- Whether aspects apply to nodes is debated in KP literature. — [IndiaDivine](https://www.indiadivine.org/content/topic/1435089-rahuketu-as-significators-different-views/)

**Modern opinion**
- Commercial sites present Venus–Rahu as a period of relationships, marriage, foreign ties, unconventional unions — unverified popular claims. — [Astromedha](https://astromedha.in/insights/vedic/rahu-mahadasha-venus-antardasha); [Kalmanas](https://www.kalmanas.com/dasha/venus-rahu-antardasha)

### Inferences
- The given Rahu signification set (12, 6, 10) is already the output of the agent rule; to interpret, the writer should name which agent (conjunct/aspect/star/sign lord) supplied each house, since the KP texts let the node's agent-houses override its own.
- The BPHS Venus–Rahu verse does not mention marriage/wife explicitly in the snippets found; using it for an engagement relies on the general "enjoyment" (bhoga) language plus the house-placement condition.

## Q4. Jupiter antardasha in Venus mahadasha for marriage

### Takeaway
BPHS (Venus antardaśā chapter, cited as ch. 61 vv. 54–59 in one edition) gives favourable Jupiter-in-Venus results such as regaining a "lost kingdom" when Jupiter is well placed; modern writers widely list Venus–Jupiter as a classic marriage window, tempered by the Jupiter–Venus natural enmity and Venus's condition.

### Cited Findings
- BPHS location and content: "In the Chapter number 61, 'Shukara Anter Dasha Phala Adhyay' … Sloka number 54 to 59 the results of the Anter Dasha of Jupiter in the Maha Dasha of Venus have been explained"; "if Jupiter is favorably placed then it can give native 'regain of lost kingdom'." — [Astrological Musings, Enigmas of Jupiter–Venus dasha](https://astrologicalmusings.com/enigmas-of-jupiter-venus-and-venus-jupiter-dasha-periods-i/)
- Duration: 20 × 16 / 120 ≈ 2 years 8 months. — [Jagannath Hora, Venus MD Jupiter AD](https://jagannathhora.com/venus-mahadasha-jupiter-antardasha-effects/)
- Modern: "one of the more frequently cited windows for marriage, committed partnerships, and family expansion"; with Venus afflicted it can bring "relational difficulties or delayed marriage timing." — [Astromedha, Venus MD Jupiter AD](https://astromedha.in/insights/vedic/venus-mahadasha-jupiter-antardasha)
- Venus–Jupiter, Jupiter–Venus, or 7th-lord–Venus periods listed as classic marriage combinations. — [Dekho Panchang, learn module 14-3](https://dekhopanchang.com/learn/modules/14-3)
- Natural enmity: classical sources class Jupiter and Venus as mutual enemies, though modern writers say the lived expression "rarely feels adversarial." — [Astromedha](https://astromedha.in/insights/vedic/venus-mahadasha-jupiter-antardasha); [Jagannath Hora](https://jagannathhora.com/venus-mahadasha-jupiter-antardasha-effects/)

### Inferences
- Jupiter (kāraka of husband for a female chart, of dharma/sanctification generally) following Rahu in Venus MD is commonly read in modern practice as the period where a relationship gets sanctified/formalised — but whether it does depends on Jupiter's KP significations (2/7/11 vs 1/6/10/12) and its sub lord; nothing here substitutes for the engine's Jupiter data.

### Gaps
- Exact BPHS verse wording of Venus–Jupiter (wife/marriage mention, if any) not verified; chapter numbering differs across editions.
- No Phaladīpikā, Raman, K.N. Rao or Sanjay Rath statement specific to Venus–Jupiter for marriage found.
