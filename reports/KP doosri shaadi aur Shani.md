# Venus vaada kholta hai, Shani atkaata hai

**Seedha jawab:** is chart mein KP ke hisaab se shaadi ka **vaada hai, inkaar nahi**, kyunki 7th cusp sub-lord (CSL) **Venus** hai aur woh houses **1, 2, 7** signify karta hai. 2 aur 7 dono fulfilment houses hain, aur KP mein inkaar tabhi hota hai jab 2, 7, 11 mein se ek bhi signify na ho. Isliye "zindagi bhar single rahega" wala reading is data se **support nahi hota**. Lekin *doosri* shaadi ke liye jo alag "plurality" markers maange jaate hain (dual-sign rule, 2nd CSL ka 7/11 se link), woh **is chart mein pass nahi hote**. Isliye KP mein doosri shaadi ka vaada "khula hai par saaf likha hua nahi", saaf "nahi" bhi nahi. June 2026 ki engagement par Shani (pratyantar lord) ka asar **mixed** hai. Shani 11 deta hai, jo event ko trigger karta hai, par saath mein 10, 12, 8 bhi deta hai, jo rukawat aur baad ki tootan ke houses hain. D1 mein Shani apni rashi mein hai, par D9 mein neech hai aur Rahu ke saath hai. Isliye use saaf "fixer" kehna doctrine se zyada bolna hoga. Jaimini mein pehli shaadi ke UL ke 2nd ghar par sirf malefic drishti hai, jo tootan ke itihaas se mel khaata hai. Doosre spouse ke point (UL2 = Gemini) ke 2nd ghar Cancer mein **uchcha Guru** baitha hai, aur yahi is chart ka sabse bada "bachaav" signal hai. Rahu AD (Feb 2025–Feb 2028) KP mein negating bhukti hai. Jupiter AD (Feb 2028–Oct 2030) zyada sahayak hai, aur usi ke andar 7th house/7th lord par Guru-Shani ka double-transit padta hai. **Sabse badi chetavani:** saare KP nishkarsh birth time par tike hain. 7th CSL sirf ~06:21–06:25 ke beech Venus hai, isliye ±2 minute ki galti poora KP faisla badal sakti hai.

## Birth time ±2 minute poore KP faisle ko palat sakta hai

Yeh baat sabse pehle, kyunki baaki sab isi par tika hai. Engine ke mutabik 7th CSL **sirf 06:21 se 06:25 ke beech Venus** hai. Birth time 4 minute pehle ho to CSL **Ketu (4, 9, 10)** ban jaata hai, aur 4 minute baad ho to **Sun (1, 3, 4, 12, 6)** (engine). Dono vikalpon mein 2, 7, 11 mein se ek bhi nahi hai. KP ke standard niyam mein yeh **inkaar wala pattern** hai: "Denial requires that none of 2, 7 or 11 is signified" ([astrogle](https://www.astrogle.com/astrology/8th-house-for-marriage-or-divorce-in-kp.html)). Isi tarah 2nd CSL 06:23–06:29 ke beech **Mercury (4, 1, 3)** hai, aur 06:13–06:21 ke beech **Saturn (8, 5, 10, 11, 12)** (engine). Iska matlab yeh hai ki agar janam ka samay ghadi se nahi, andaze se likha gaya tha, to neeche ka KP vishleshan ek *sambhav* chart ka hai, pakka nahi.

Ek baat dhyaan dene layak hai, par isse koi calibration nahi kiya gaya. Native ki ek shaadi ho chuki hai. Ketu ya Sun wale vikalp KP mein inkaar dikhate, jabki Venus wala vikalp vaada dikhata hai. Matlab jeevan ki ghatnaayein 06:21–06:25 wali khidki se *mel khaati hain*. Lekin yeh rectification nahi hai. Proper rectification ke bina birth time ko "confirmed" nahi maana ja sakta.

## Venus 1-2-7 vaada deta hai, par doosri shaadi ke markers fail hote hain

### Pehli shaadi ka vaada aur uski tootan dono 7th CSL mein dikhte hain

Sabhi sources maante hain ki marriage ka mukhya judge **7th CSL** hai, aur uska 2, 7, 11 signify karna vaada deta hai ([jagannathhora 2-7-11](https://jagannathhora.com/timing-marriage-2-7-11-formula/)). KSK ke naam se (secondhand) yeh bhi kaha gaya hai ki ek se zyada shaadi ke liye "the only rule to be followed is to note the sub-lord of the 7th cusp" ([astrogle](https://www.astrogle.com/astrology/second-marriage-divorce-in-astrology.html)). Yahan 7th cusp Libra 3°37' hai, jiska star Mars aur **sub-lord Venus** hai. Venus Aries 25°47' par apne hi nakshatra Bharani mein baitha hai, sub Mercury (engine). Venus **1, 2, 7** signify karta hai, yaani 2 aur 7 fulfilment, aur 1 negation (1 = 2 ka 12th). Practitioner Mitra ke anusaar agar 7th CSL 1, 6 ya 10 bhi signify kare to "divorce or break in marriage is clearly promised" ([LinkedIn, Mitra](https://www.linkedin.com/pulse/promise-marriage-second-alongwith-marital-issues-kp-mitra-fca)). To yeh ek **"shaadi hogi, aur ek tootan bhi likhi hai"** wala pattern hai, jo 2021 ki tooti engagement, Jan 2024 ki shaadi aur Mar 2025/Feb 2026 ki tootan ke itihaas se mel khaata hai. Kuch practitioners 2nd house ko 7th se 8th (spouse ki aayu/ant) bhi padhte hain. Unke hisaab se 7th CSL ka 2 se link pehli shaadi ke *ant* ka raasta dikhata hai, jo doosri ke liye raasta kholta hai, use mana nahi karta ([Scribd 2nd marriage divorce](https://www.scribd.com/document/592038252/2nd-marriage-divorce); [heavenschild](https://www.heavenschild.com.au/k-astrology/kp-astrology-second-marriage.html)).

Ek alag test 11th CSL se divorce dekhta hai. Agar 11th CSL 6 ya 10 signify kare to divorce ([Quora](https://www.quora.com/How-do-you-know-that-native-married-life-will-be-separated-or-divorced-by-KP-astrology)). Yahan 11th CSL bhi Venus (1, 2, 7) hai, jo 6 ya 10 nahi deta. Is ek practitioner test ke hisaab se *formal* divorce ki jagah alagaav/tootan zyada fit baithta hai. Yeh ek akele source ka test hai, isliye kamzor maana jaaye.

### Plurality test: teeno raaste band dikhte hain

Ek vaada apne aap kai shaadiyon ka licence nahi hai. Doosri shaadi ke apne markers chahiye ([jagannathhora, Second Marriage](https://jagannathhora.com/second-marriage-predictions-kp/)). KSK ke naam wala dual-sign niyam kehta hai ki 7th CSL "Mercury ho, ya dual sign mein ho, ya dual sign mein baithe graha ke nakshatra mein ho" ([astrogle](https://www.astrogle.com/astrology/second-marriage-divorce-in-astrology.html)). AstroSage isme dusri shart bhi jodta hai ki 7th CSL 2 ya 11 bhi signify kare, aur dono shartein ek saath poori honi chahiye ([AstroSage KP 7th House](https://kpastrology.astrosage.com/kp-learning-home/kp-rules/7th-house)).

| Test | Is chart mein | Natija |
|---|---|---|
| 7th CSL = Mercury? | Venus | Fail |
| 7th CSL dual sign mein? | Aries (movable) | Fail |
| CSL ka star-lord dual sign mein / dual sign ka maalik? | Star-lord Venus khud, Aries mein; Venus Taurus/Libra ka maalik | Fail |
| 7th CSL 2 ya 11 signify kare? (AstroSage ki dusri shart) | 2 haan | Pass, par pehli shart fail hai |
| 2nd CSL 7 ya 11 signify kare? (astrogle Rule 2) | Mercury → 4, 1, 3 | Fail |
| 2nd CSL 2/7/11 signify kare? (Jagannath Hora) | Mercury → 4, 1, 3 | Fail |
| 9th CSL (fringe Rule 3) | Venus → 1, 2, 7 | Support (agar 2 ko fulfilment maanein) |

Saar yeh hai ki **main-stream plurality tests fail hote hain**, aur sirf sabse kamzor 9th-CSL test support karta hai. 9th CSL test ko sources khud "less standard in KP" kehte hain ([jagannathhora](https://jagannathhora.com/second-marriage-predictions-kp/)). astrogle ka 100-chart study 9th CSL ko 93% agreement deta hai, par woh study undocumented hai ([astrogle](https://www.astrogle.com/astrology/second-marriage-divorce-in-astrology.html)), isliye us number par bharosa nahi kiya ja sakta. Birth time sensitivity yahan bhi asar daalti hai. Agar 2nd CSL Saturn (06:13–06:21) ho, to woh 11 signify karta hai aur Rule 2 pass hota hai. Lekin "exclusive connection with the 11th house hints at non-marital companionship" ([Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/592038252/2nd-marriage-divorce)), yaani kaanooni shaadi nahi, saath.

**Kya woh zindagi bhar single rahega?** KP ke data se **nahi** kaha ja sakta. Inkaar ke liye 7th CSL mein sirf 1, 6, 10 hone chahiye, aur yahan 2 aur 7 maujood hain ([jagannathhora, Denial](https://jagannathhora.com/?p=346); [astrogle](https://www.astrogle.com/astrology/8th-house-for-marriage-or-divorce-in-kp.html)). "Unmarried for life" wala koi verified KSK quote bhi nahi mila. Sahi phrasing yeh hai ki **shaadi ka vaada saabit ho chuka hai, aur doosri shaadi ke liye strict (AstroSage-type) niyam "nahi" ki taraf jhukta hai, jabki loose (KSK "sirf 7th CSL dekho" + 9th CSL) reading use khula chhodti hai.** Notes ka nirdesh hai ki dono views alag-alag rakhe jaayein, unhe ek faisle mein na milaya jaaye.

## Pichhla jawab sahi nateeje par galat raaste se pahuncha tha

Is conversation mein pehle kaha gaya tha: *"KP mein doosri shaadi ka saaf vaada nahi hai"*, aur iska aadhaar engine ka second-marriage profile tha, jo **9th cusp** dekhta hai aur **2 ko negation** maanta hai. Notes ke hisaab se ismein do doctrinal galtiyan thi. Pehli galti: mainstream KP mein **2 fulfilment house hai**. Negation set 1, 6, 10 hai (2, 7, 11 ke 12th), aur kisi bhi source ne doosri shaadi ke liye 2 ko negation nahi maana ([AstroSage Ch. 2](https://kpastrology.astrosage.com/kp-learning-home/tutorial/chapter-2-fundamental-principles)). Dusri galti: **mukhya judge 7th CSL hai, 9th CSL nahi.** 9th CSL fringe aur assumption-based hai ([astrogle](https://www.astrogle.com/astrology/second-marriage-divorce-in-astrology.html)). Ulti baat yeh hai ki agar 9th CSL ko 2 = fulfilment maan kar padha jaaye, to Venus (1, 2, 7) doosri shaadi ko *support* karta hai, inkaar nahi karta.

**Sudhra hua kathan:** "KP mein shaadi ka vaada hai (7th CSL Venus, 2 aur 7). Doosri shaadi ke vishesh plurality markers (dual-sign rule aur 2nd CSL) is chart mein nahi milte, isliye vaada *saaf likha hua* nahi hai. Lekin inkaar bhi nahi hai, aur 'zindagi bhar akela' ka koi KP aadhaar nahi hai." Nateeja kuch had tak wahi hai, par vajah badal gayi hai. Engine ke second-marriage profile ka "2 = negation" wala logic `docs/RULE_CHANGELOG.md` ke process ke tahat review hona chahiye. Yeh engine ke doctrine ka sawaal hai, isliye isse approval ke liye alag se uthaya jaana chahiye.

## Shani ki engagement: 11 ka trigger, par 10-12-8 ka bojh

June 2026 ki engagement **Venus MD / Rahu AD / Saturn PD** (PD 22 Mar–2 Sep 2026) mein hui (engine). KP mein Shani **8, 5, 10, 11, 12** signify karta hai. Ismein 11 ichha-poorti hai aur 5 romance. 10 negation set mein hai (11 ka 12th), aur 8 aur 12 ko kai writers *shaadi ke baad* ki tootan ke houses maante hain ([Paramarsh](https://paramarsh.app/patrika/kp-astrology/kp-marriage-timing)). Notes ke anusaar yeh "textbook mixed significator" hai: **event trigger hota hai (11), par rukawat ya asthirta ke saath (10, 12, 8).** Shani na 2 deta hai na 7. KP mein antim faisla Shani ke *sub-lord* ka hota hai ("When the star lord and sub lord disagree, the sub lord prevails" ([RedAstrologer](https://redastrologer.com/what-is-sub-lord-in-kp-astrology-sub-lord-table/))), aur **Shani ka sub-lord engine data mein nahi diya gaya.** Isliye KP ka last word yahan khula hai. Upar se poori engagement **Rahu AD** ke neeche hui, jo 12, 6, 10 signify karta hai aur 2, 7, 11 mein se kuch nahi. Niyam ke hisaab se yeh marriage ke liye negating bhukti hai, aur engine ka divorce profile bhi Rahu AD ko "rupture ki taraf" dikhata hai.

"Shani deri karta hai par sthirta deta hai" wali baat **modern consensus** hai. Ise B.V. Raman ke naam se secondary blogs mein diya jaata hai ([Astroshastra](https://www.astroshastra.com/Marriage%20Astrology/latemarriage.php)). Koi classical shloka nahi mila jo kahe ki Shani shaadi ko "sambhalta" hai. Classical roop se Shani sirf āyuṣkāraka hai ([Light in Life](https://lightinlife.home.blog/2021/04/19/combinations-for-longevity-part-1/)). Modern sources bhi yeh sthirta **Shani ki dignity par shart** rakhte hain. Neech (Aries) ya pīḍit Shani zyada pareshaan karta hai, aur Navāṁśa mein kamzor Shani shaadi ko "emotionally dry or burdened" banata hai ([Astrologylover](https://astrologylover.com/saturn-in-7th-house-in-navamsa-chart-or-d9-chart/)). Is chart mein dono taraf ke tathya hain. D1 mein Shani **swagrihi (Capricorn, 10H)** hai, jo sthirta ke paksh mein hai. Lekin D9 mein **neech (Aries)** hai, D1 mein **Rahu ke saath** hai, aur 7th house par graha-drishti daal raha hai (engine). Jaimini mein Shani doosre spouse ke sustenance point (Cancer) par bhi drishti daalta hai (neeche dekhein).

**Faisla (b):** doctrine ke hisaab se Shani ko is engagement ka **saaf "fixer" nahi kaha ja sakta**. Uska 11 batata hai ki commitment *bana*, jo hua bhi. Lekin 10, 12, 8, D9 ki neechta, Rahu ki yuti aur Rahu AD ka negating background milkar **rukawat, deri ya baad mein dabaav** ki taraf zyada jhukte hain. "Fixed-then-delayed/cancelled" wala reading is shape se mel khaata hai ([notes Q2 inference; Jagannath Hora](https://jagannathhora.com/kp-significator-hierarchy-4-level-reading/)), par yeh inference hai, niyam nahi. Saturn PD 2 Sep 2026 ko khatam ho chuki hai, isliye ab us engagement ki disha agle sub-periods aur Jupiter AD tay karenge. Do aur baatein bhi hain. H9 par double-transit (Dec 2025–Jun 2026) engagement ke samay ke aas-paas padta hai, par 9th ko "doosra spouse" maanna sirf modern derivation hai. Aur 2026 ka Tajika Muntha 12th mein hai, lekin notes mein Tajika ka koi doctrine nahi hai, isliye use yahan interpret nahi kiya ja raha.

## Parashari aur Jaimini: pehli shaadi ki tootan saaf, doosre spouse ko Guru ka kavach

### Parashari: tootan ke yoga zyada, "do patni" ke seedhe yoga kam

Classical "ek se zyada shaadi" ke yogon mein sabse saaf yoga hai Venus aur 7th lord ka **dual rashi ya dual amsha** mein hona ([Phaladeepika Ch.10, wisdomlib](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/phaladeepika-by-mantreswara-text-and-translation/d/doc1621582.html)). Yahan 7th lord khud Venus hai, D1 mein **Aries (chara)** aur D9 mein **Scorpio (sthira)** mein, isliye yeh yoga **nahi banta**. BPHS 18.21 (strong 7th lord ke saath Venus dual sign mein) bhi nahi banta ([enjoylearningsanskrit 18.21](https://enjoylearningsanskrit.com/scriptures/parashara/chapter-18/verse-21)). Phaladeepika ka "eunuch graha 7th mein + 11th mein do graha" bhi nahi banta, kyunki dono ghar khaali hain.

Jo bante hain, woh zyadatar **pehle spouse ki haani ya alagaav** ke yoga hain. Phaladeepika ke mutabik "malefics in the 12th, 4th or 8th from Venus" aur "7th ya 7th lord par malefic drishti" se patni ki haani hoti hai ([wisdomlib](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/phaladeepika-by-mantreswara-text-and-translation/d/doc1621582.html)). Is chart mein Venus se 12th (Pisces) mein Sun hai, 4th (Cancer) mein Ketu hai (Jupiter ke saath), aur 7th par Shani ki drishti hai (engine). Notes ka zor yahi hai ki aise yoga "pehli shaadi tooti" dikhate hain, "doosri pakki" nahi, aur in dono baaton ko alag rakhna zaroori hai.

Saravali v.47 ke ek anuvaad ke anusaar patniyon ki sankhya **7th ko dekhne wale grahon** ki sankhya se judti hai ([jyotisa.wordpress](https://jyotisa.wordpress.com/2013/11/30/the-corpus-of-kalyana-varma-a-k-a-saravali/)). Yahan 7th par teen grahon (Mercury, Venus, Saturn) ki drishti hai. Yeh ek se zyada rishte ki taraf ishaara karta hai, par anuvaad aur verse numbering dono anishchit hain. Saravali v.48 (7th mein Venus + Moon se bahu-patni) D1 par lagu nahi hota. D9 ke 7th (Scorpio, Taurus lagna se) mein Venus + Moon baithe hain, lekin D1 ka niyam D9 par lagana **meri extrapolation** hai, shastra nahi. Isi verse ka "single wife" condition (Leo/Aries/Scorpio navāṁśa) bhi Scorpio par ulta ishaara de sakta hai. Isliye Saravali yahan donon taraf khinchta hai aur ise **anishchit** maana jaaye. Venus ka combust hona BPHS ke "kamzor 7th lord" test ke liye zaroori hai, par Sun ka degree data mein nahi hai, isliye use judge nahi kiya gaya.

Doosre spouse ka ghar kaunsa hai, is par sources mein matbhed hai. **Uttara Kalamrita** natal **8th** ghar kehta hai ([Sastri tr.](https://dn721906.ca.archive.org/0/items/kupdf.net_uttkalamrita-kalidas-ps-sastri/kupdf.net_uttkalamrita-kalidas-ps-sastri.pdf)), aur **Sanjay Rath** 7th se 8th yaani natal **2nd** ([Scribd, Rath](https://www.scribd.com/document/265405739/Marriage-Sanjay-Rath)). 8th (Scorpio) mein **neech Moon** hai, jo bhavnaatmak kamzori dikhata hai, par us par **Guru ki drishti** hai, jo bachaav karti hai. 2nd (Taurus) khaali hai aur uska lord Venus lagna (kendra) mein apne nakshatra mein hai, jo madhyam-se-theek sthiti hai (engine). Dono reading mein inkaar nahi hai, aur 8th wali reading mein Guru ka sahara hai.

### Jaimini: UL ka 2nd khaali-aur-malefic, UL2 ka 2nd uchcha-Guru wala

Jaimini/BPHS 30 mein **UL ka 2nd ghar** shaadi ko tikata ya todta hai. Wahan malefic ho aur benefic drishti na ho to tootan hoti hai, aur benefic yuti ya drishti tootan rokti hai ([shrifreedom.org](https://shrifreedom.org/vedic-astrology/upapada-lagna/)). Is chart mein UL Scorpio hai, aur uska 2nd **Sagittarius** **khaali hai, Mars (graha + rashi drishti) aur Sun (rashi drishti) se dekha ja raha hai, aur us par koi benefic drishti nahi hai** (engine). Yeh pehli shaadi ki tootan ka classic Jaimini signature hai aur itihaas se mel khaata hai. Ek nuance: yaaddasht par aadharit (unverified) sūtra 1.4.4 kehta hai ki Sun ko malefic nahi gina jaata agar woh mitra rashi mein ho. Sun Pisces (Guru ki rashi) mein hai, isliye agar yeh sūtra sahi hai to asli tootne wala graha **Mars** bachta hai.

Rath school mein agla spouse **UL se 8th** (UL2) se dekha jaata hai ([Scribd, Rath](https://www.scribd.com/document/265405739/Marriage-Sanjay-Rath)). Yahan UL2 = **Gemini** hai, jismein Mars baitha hai (engine). Sources ke anusaar 8th-from-UL "describes the character of a later relationship *if one occurs*", yaani yeh khud doosri shaadi ki guarantee nahi deta ([jagannathhora](https://jagannathhora.com/upapada-lagna-spouse-appearance/)). Durability ke liye UL2 ka 2nd (**Cancer**, natal UL se 9th) dekhna padega. Yeh rule notes mein Rath ki chaining se nikala gaya *inference* hai, quoted niyam nahi. Cancer mein **uchcha Guru + Ketu** hain, aur Shani aur Rahu ki graha-drishti aur Moon ki rashi-drishti hai (engine). Doctrine ke hisaab se iske teen hisse hain:

| Kaarak | Doctrine | Is chart mein asar |
|---|---|---|
| Uchcha Guru 2nd-from-UL2 mein | "Jupiter in 2nd from UL prevents divorce"; benefic se lambi shaadi ([star-meet](https://star-meet.com/blog/upapada-lagna-vedic-astrology-spouse); [astroword](https://www.astroword.in/upapada-lagna)) | Sabse mazboot bachaav |
| Uchcha graha wahan | "Exalted planet in 2nd from UL gives many wives" / "ya woh dirghayu hoti hai" ([Science of Light](https://www.scienceoflight.net/science-of-light-course/classical-vedic-astrology/parashara-and-jaimini/)) | Paradox: sthirta *ya* aur rishte. Dono padhne honge |
| Shani + Rahu ki drishti, Ketu yuti | Saturn/Rahu → scandal, calamity se haani; benefic narm karta hai ([shrifreedom](https://shrifreedom.org/vedic-astrology/upapada-lagna/)) | Dabaav aur rukawat, par Guru andar baitha hai |

Notes ka sabse kaam ka triangulation signal yeh hai ki agar doosri shaadi tikne wali ho to uske sustenance point par woh benefic suraksha dikhni chahiye jo pehle UL ke 2nd par *nahi thi*. **Is chart mein yeh contrast saaf maujood hai.** Pehle UL ke 2nd par koi benefic nahi hai, jabki UL2 ke 2nd mein uchcha Guru hai. Phir bhi Shani-Rahu ki drishti ise "bina sangharsh ki" shaadi nahi rehne deti. Isliye **(c) ka jawab** yeh hai ki Jaimini doosre spouse ko pehle se zyada suraksha deta hai, par woh suraksha dabaav ke beech hai. Notes kehte hain ki koi classical verse *doosri* shaadi ki durability par seedha nahi bolta, aur yeh sab pehli shaadi ke niyamon ka vistaar hai. Ek aur baat: Cancer = A7 (Darapada) bhi hai, par notes mein A7 ka doctrine nahi hai, isliye ise sirf ek observation maana jaaye. Dārakāraka Mercury hai. Ek modern lekh doosri shaadi ko DK ke "curriculum" ki punaravriti maanta hai ([jagannathhora](https://jagannathhora.com/tag/marriage-astrology/)), jo Mercury AD (Dec 2033–Oct 2036) ko dhyaan dene layak banata hai. Yeh modern raay hai, niyam nahi.

## Rahu AD todti hai, Jupiter AD "khoya raajya" lautaati hai

**Rahu AD (18 Feb 2025–18 Feb 2028):** KP mein Rahu **12, 6, 10** signify karta hai, jismein 2, 7, 11 mein se kuch nahi. Niyam ke hisaab se yeh marriage ke liye negating bhukti hai. Engine ke chain counts (Venus-Rahu PDs mein ~2–3 fulfilment vs 4–5 negation) aur divorce profile ("rupture ki taraf") bhi yahi dikhate hain. Alagaav (Mar 2025) aur tootan (Feb 2026) dono isi AD mein hue. KP mein Rahu apne saathi graha ka agent hota hai ("Rahu or Ketu will act as a strong agent to the planets to which they are conjoined" ([IndiaDivine, KP Reader II](https://www.indiadivine.org/content/topic/1435089-rahuketu-as-significators-different-views/))). Yahan Rahu Shani ke saath aur Shani ki rashi mein hai, lekin engine ki Rahu list mein Shani ka 11 nahi dikhta. **Kaunsa agent kaunsa house de raha hai, yeh engine ne nahi bataya.** Is par doctrine (agent order) khud vivaadit hai, isliye yeh ek khula sawaal hai. BPHS ki Venus-Rahu shart, yaani Rahu kendra/trikona/11th mein ho to "great enjoyment, gain of wealth, visits of friends" ([barbarapijan BPHS](https://barbarapijan.com/bpa/VimshottariDasha/Zukra_Mahadasha_BPHS.htm)), yahan aanshik roop se poori hoti hai kyunki Rahu 10H (kendra) mein hai. Lekin us verse mein shaadi ka seedha zikr nahi mila, aur Rahu ki malefic Shani se yuti hai. To Parashari reading Rahu AD ko "bhog aur sambandh ka samay, par asthir" deti hai, aur KP use saaf negating deta hai. Dono milkar Rahu AD ko **rishte banne ka samay maante hain, tikne ka nahi**. Engagement isi AD mein hai, isliye uske tikne ki parakh Feb 2028 ke baad hogi.

**Jupiter AD (18 Feb 2028–19 Oct 2030):** KP mein Guru **10, 11, 12, 4, 9** signify karta hai. Ismein 11 fulfilment hai, 10 negation, aur 9 woh fringe "another spouse" house hai. Engine ke chain counts (~3–5 vs 3) zyada santulit hain, aur divorce profile "rupture se door" hai. Guru ka sub-lord data mein nahi hai, isliye KP ka antim shabd phir bhi khula hai. BPHS (Venus antardasha chapter, ek edition mein ch. 61 vv. 54–59) kehta hai ki Guru shubh sthiti mein ho to "regain of lost kingdom" deta hai ([Astrological Musings](https://astrologicalmusings.com/enigmas-of-jupiter-venus-and-venus-jupiter-dasha-periods-i/)). Is chart mein Guru **uchcha, kendra (4H)** mein hai, jo shubh sthiti hai. Modern sources Venus-Jupiter ko "one of the more frequently cited windows for marriage" maante hain, par Guru-Venus ki naisargik shatruta ki chetavani ke saath ([Astromedha](https://astromedha.in/insights/vedic/venus-mahadasha-jupiter-antardasha)). Gochara bhi isi AD ke saath mel khaata hai. 7th house aur Venus par Guru-Shani double-transit **Dec 2028–Mar 2029** aur **Oct 2029–Jan 2030** mein padta hai, aur Venus par **Feb–Jul 2028** mein, jo AD ki shuruaat ke saath hai. Notes ke "events cluster at daśā onsets" wale siddhant ke hisaab se Feb 2028 ka AD-onset dhyaan dene layak hai. 2028 ka Tajika Muntha 2nd (lord Venus) mein hai. KP mein 2 parivaar-vriddhi ka ghar hai, par notes mein Tajika doctrine nahi hai, isliye ise sirf saath-chalta observation maana jaaye. (Timing ke liye `docs/AI_EVENT_TIMING_GUIDE.md` lagu hota hai, aur KP timing ka judge nahi hai.)

Iske baad bhi double-transit windows hain: Saturn AD (Dec 2033 tak), phir **Mercury (Dārakāraka) AD mein Apr 2035–Apr 2036**, aur 2036–38 aur 2041–43 mein. Yeh "zindagi bhar akela" wale reading ke khilaaf ek aur data point hai, kyunki gochara baar-baar 7th ko trigger karta rehta hai.

## Conclusion

Is chart ka asli sawaal "shaadi likhi hai ya nahi" nahi hai. Woh to likhi thi aur ho bhi chuki hai. Asli sawaal yeh hai ki **doosri shaadi ka KP mein koi vishesh marker na hone ke bawajood baaki schools use kitna sahara dete hain.** Teeno schools ek alag-alag pattern dikhate hain. KP tootan ko saaf dikhata hai par doosri shaadi par "chup" hai. Parashari zyadatar pehli tootan ke yoga deta hai. Jaimini akela aisa school hai jo doosre spouse ke point par pehle se *zyada* suraksha (uchcha Guru) dikhata hai. Isliye sabse imaandaar triangulated raay yeh hai: **doosra rishta sambhav hai aur use tikne ka sahara hai, par KP ki taraf se vaada kamzor hai, aur Shani-Rahu ka dabaav use mehnat waala banata hai.** Shani ko "engagement pakki karne wala" maanna doctrine se aage jaana hoga. Is chart mein Shani ki ek hi saaf bhoomika hai, aur woh Rahu AD ke dauraan rukawat aur deri ki hai.

Aage ke liye do kaam is reading ko kisi bhi bhavishyavaani se zyada mazboot banayenge. Pehla, **birth time ki pushti ya rectification**, kyunki 7th CSL ka Venus se Ketu ya Sun ho jaana KP ka poora jawab ulat deta hai. Dusra, engine se **Shani, Rahu aur Guru ke sub-lords** aur Rahu ke agent-breakdown nikalna, kyunki KP mein antim faisla wahi karte hain aur woh abhi data mein nahi hain. Saath hi engine ke second-marriage profile ka "2 = negation" wala logic notes ke mainstream KP doctrine se takraata hai.
